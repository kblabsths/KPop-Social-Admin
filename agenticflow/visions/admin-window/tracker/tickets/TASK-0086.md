1. 2. A performer reference field, overridden the same way, produces event_performers ROWS — read back by event id, asserted against the linked entity's id, never a text column.

4. Each of the two overrides appends exactly one verdicts row with action = override, read back by id.
5. The display half still holds after the write: the reference renders as a link to the record it names, not a bare uuid and not text.
6. The world is READ, not assumed: in the absent or half-installed world both cases skip with the reason named, and the existing arms of the two live files are unchanged and still pass.
7. Before-state and after-state of every row and link this test creates are recorded in the evidence note, restorable by the human who owns those rows, and the residue sweep is green at the end of the run.
