# Two figures on /claims never silently disagree: each says whether it is a window or a total

## Description
**Modules:** `src/app/claims/page.tsx`, `src/components/claims/**` (the bucket table and the gauge cards' labels), `tests/offline/claims/page.test.ts`, `tests/live/claims.live.test.ts`.

**The defect (SPEC F15, M3 EC7).** Two sets of per-bucket figures stand on one page and are read two different ways: the bucket table's counts are `head: true, count: "exact"` reads of the WHOLE narrowing (`readClaimCount`), and the Claims tab gauge's bucket distribution is computed over a WINDOW of at most 1,000 rows. Past 1,000 claims they diverge with nothing on screen saying so. Staging is at 877 — this is live within months, not theoretical.

**The ruling: LABEL, do not equalise.** Making them equal has only two forms and both are worse. Capping the head counts to the window would make the page's totals wrong rather than windowed. Scanning the whole view to fill the gauge is the ~14-round-trip, 2.9-3.8 s read BUG-0138 removed and Ben complained about by name. So: **each figure says, in RENDERED TEXT, what kind of fact it is** — a window's, or the narrowing's total — and no sentence on the page asserts a relationship between two figures that no single read established (LESSONS 2).

The page already owns part of this vocabulary and it is the vocabulary to extend, not to reinvent: `LIST_COUNT_EYEBROW` ("Matching claims"), `POPULATION_EYEBROW` ("Whole-view count") and `GAUGE_POPULATION_EYEBROW` ("Window population"). The gauge's distribution card and the bucket table's caption are what lack it.

**What must be true of the rendered HTML** (asserted on markup, never on the read — M3 EC7):

- On a fixture holding MORE than 1,000 claims, for every bucket that appears in both places, either the two figures are equal, or each is labelled as the kind of fact it is, in text a reader sees — not in an attribute alone, and not in a comment.
- The same fixture produces no rendered sentence asserting a relationship between two figures that no single read established (for example "N of M", a percentage, or a difference, computed across the two).
- The labelling holds on the standing tab as well as the pending one, and under a `?source_id=` / `?domain=` narrowing, where both figures move.
- The window line above the gauge keeps saying exactly what it says today — this ticket adds no window line and moves no figure.

**Voice:** the words are the designer's jurisdiction, not this ticket's criteria. Use the app's existing nouns (a window is a window, a total is a total) and keep the sentences short and quiet; a walk grades the copy, the checks grade the structure.

**No new surface, no new figure** (M3 EC10): the sidebar still holds exactly six links, `src/app` gains no page route, and no figure is added to the page — this ticket makes the ones already there say which kind they are.

## Acceptance criteria
On a fixture holding more than 1,000 claims, the Claims tab gauge's bucket figures and the head counts above them are either equal or each labelled in rendered text as the kind of fact it is — window versus total — asserted on rendered HTML for every bucket that appears in both places, on both tabs, and under a source_id and a domain narrowing. That fixture produces no rendered sentence asserting a relationship between two figures no single read established. On a fixture below the window, where the two are equal, the page renders no contradiction and no extra apology. The window line, the row set and every existing figure are unchanged from the pre-ticket tree on the same fixtures. No figure and no surface is added: the sidebar holds exactly six links and src/app gains no page route. /claims' live parity suite is green, its oracles addressed by data-surface and deciding the state kind from data-state before comparing a number.
