# The bound-ceiling sentence tells /browse's operator to narrow a view /browse offers no way to narrow

## Description
**The sentence.** `PageMore`'s bound-ceiling arm (`stoppedAtTheCeiling`,
`src/components/ui/paging.tsx:483`) ends:

> This app serves no bound past 100,000 {holds}, so paging stops here and not at
> the end of the set. **Narrow the view and page the smaller set.**

The noun is per-surface — `/browse` correctly reads "events" — but **the next
step is not**, and `/browse` offers nothing to narrow with. Its only parameter
is `columns`, which the page's own comment states *"chooses which COLUMNS render
and never which rows are read"* (`src/app/browse/page.tsx:218-221`, `scope: null`).
I confirmed it on the landed tree at 1440x900: `/browse` renders one chip row —
the column selector — and no facet of any kind; nothing in that URL removes a
row. So on the one surface where the arm's noun is "events", its instruction
tells the operator to do a thing the page does not offer.

**What it fails.** LOOK_AND_FEEL → The Voice → copy bar 3: *"Every error names
what failed, then what to do, with no apology"* — the "what to do" half is the
bar, and a next step the surface cannot honour does not meet it; it is the
wishful fix LESSONS 1 names. And the Register: *"plain, specific"* — advice the
page cannot take is neither. On `/claims` the same sentence PASSES: that surface
has `bucket` and `source_id` chips and narrowing really is how it is worked.

**Reachability, stated honestly, because it decides the priority and not the
verdict.** The arm draws only at `MAX_PAGE_OFFSET + SIZE` = 100,050 rows.
Read-only staging holds **120 events** against 877 claims, so `/browse`'s ceiling
is three orders of magnitude away and no walk, criterion or receipt can drive
that surface into this arm on this data — QA reached it only through its own
`PagingProvider` + `PageMore` probe at a typed `held`. I graded it the way the
architect's M3 walk note asked: against the Voice bars on the reachable surface
(`/claims`, where it passes) and on the component reading for `/browse` (where
it does not). **P3 because it is unreachable, filed because it is wrong.**

**The two ways out, and what each costs** (measured, so the ticket is not
scoped short):

- **A next step true of every surface, or none at all.** Cheapest: no prop, no
  wrapper change, `src/components/ui/paging.tsx` and its test only. The arm's
  other two sentences already stand on their own — they name what stopped the
  paging and that it is not the end of the set, which is copy bar 3's first
  half in full.
- **A per-surface next step.** Needs a new `PageMore` prop (a next-step string,
  or a "this surface can be narrowed" flag) threaded from **both** call sites,
  `src/components/claims/paged-claim-list.tsx:94` and
  `src/components/browse/paged-browse-table.tsx:165` — both of which were
  outside BUG-0178's touch scope, which is exactly why its builder left the gap
  rather than widening.

Both call sites are in this ticket's touch scope so either road is open to its
builder; **a fix that leaves `/browse` telling the operator to narrow an
unnarrowable view is not a fix, whichever road is taken.**

## Acceptance criteria
1. The bound-ceiling arm no longer instructs the operator of a surface to narrow
   a view that surface offers no way to narrow. Asserted on the `/browse`
   wrapper's own reading of `PageMore` at a `held` past
   `MAX_PAGE_OFFSET + SIZE`, in `tests/offline/ui/paging.test.ts`.
2. Copy bar 3's first half is untouched: the arm still names what stopped the
   paging — this app, and its own `MAX_PAGE_OFFSET`, imported and rendered
   through the app's thousands-separated count — and still says, in so many
   words, that this is not the end of the set.
3. The arm still draws **no control** (a press cannot be honoured here), still
   asserts nothing about the read, and still does not duplicate the window
   line's closing clause.
4. `/claims`' reading of the same arm still offers a next step its surface can
   honour, whether that is the sentence it carries today or a per-surface one.
5. Whichever road is taken, `MAX_PAGE_OFFSET` is imported and never retyped, and
   no new wording is pinned byte-for-byte in a test that is not this arm's own.
6. The exhausted arm, the loading arm, the idle arm and the refusal are
   byte-identical on both surfaces: this ticket moves one arm's second sentence
   and nothing else.
7. Human-check: drive `/browse` into the ceiling arm through a component
   reading at a typed `held` and read the sentence aloud — every instruction in
   it is something `/browse` actually offers. **(human-checkable)**
