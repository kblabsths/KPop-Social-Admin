# Cycles & runs names one state two ways on one screen: the rows say 'died', the health panel says 'unfinished'

## Description
Found by the document-blind user-sim walk (Tomas, 2026-09-03), `agenticflow/tracker/evidence/user-sim-m1/tomas.md`, Claim 2. Confirmed live on :8771 and at source, 2026-09-03.

**Voice glossary:** "one name per concept, everywhere" — "two builders who never meet must write the same labels."

On `/cycles`, the same four cycles are named twice, two different ways, on one screen:

- the table rows call them **`died`** — `<Badge tone="broken">died</Badge>` (`src/app/cycles/page.tsx:261-264`), from `cycleState()`'s `{ kind: "died" }` (`src/lib/db/cycles.ts:223, 261`);
- the cycle-health panel below calls them **`unfinished`** — `outcomeRows()`'s fourth known key (`src/app/cycles/page.tsx:478`), from `cycle-health.ts:134,156` where a null outcome is bucketed as `unfinished`.

Confirmed in the delivered HTML of `/cycles`: `data-cycle-state="died"` on the rows, `data-outcome-count="unfinished"` reading `4` in the panel. Same four cycles, same page, two words.

Tomas: "The panel calls four cycles **unfinished**; the rows themselves call them **died**. Same four cycles, two different words on the same screen. **I had to satisfy myself they were the same set before I'd trust the count.**"

That cost is the whole point of the glossary rule. This page's argument is that its summaries are computed from its rows — Tomas re-added all 69 rows by hand and found not one cell off, and called it "the strongest evidence in the tool that the summaries are computed from the rows and not from somewhere else". Naming the same set two ways is the one thing that made him doubt an arithmetic he had just verified.

Neither word is a machine identifier the database wrote: `resolution_runs.outcome` is NULL for these rows (`cycles.ts:214` — "`died` — no `ended_at`, and the cycle started longer ago than that"). Both words are the app's own, invented at two different call sites. So Voice bar 5 (machine identifiers verbatim in mono) does not protect either spelling — the app is free to pick one, and must.

## Acceptance criteria
`/cycles` names the no-end-past-one-cadence state with ONE word, used by both the table rows and the cycle-health panel. Which word is the build's call (`died` is the more specific and is what the row badge already carries), but the two must agree, verbatim, on the same screen.

The other three outcome words are untouched: `succeeded`, `failed`, `skipped` are the database's own `outcome` values and keep rendering verbatim (Voice bar 5).

The still-running state (no end, younger than one cadence) keeps its own distinct word — three states, three words, no two sharing a spelling and no state losing one.

The counts do not change: the panel still reads 4 for that state and the table still shows the same four rows.

`tests/offline/cycles/page.test.ts` and `tests/offline/gauges/cycle-health.test.ts` assert the row badge text and the panel's outcome label are the same string.

Human-check: on `/cycles`, read the badge on a no-end row and the fourth line of the outcome panel — same word. (human-checkable)
