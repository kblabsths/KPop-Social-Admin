# The account's one derivation widens from 'a document' to 'not the database's own words': a runtime's stack frames and a body we had to serialise

## Description
**Module:** `src/lib/db/result.ts` — `errorMessage`'s part assembly and nothing else — plus the three offline suites that pin what an account carries. `classify`, `messageOf`'s raw string, `withoutSecrets`-last, `machine.ts` and `error-line.tsx` are untouched, exactly as admin-window/BUG-0170 required.

**Found** (QA, admin-window/BUG-0170 close, residuals 1-3, measured through the real `@supabase/supabase-js` client with a stubbed fetch and — for (c) — at `/claims` with `SUPABASE_URL` pointed at `db.invalid`):

- (a) an intermediary answering **2xx with a non-JSON body** makes postgrest-js's `JSON.parse` throw; postgrest-js wraps it as `{message: "SyntaxError: …", details: <err.stack>, hint: "", code: ""}`, so the account becomes a **697-802 character stack** quoting a document fragment (`Unexpected token '<', "<!DOCTYPE "…`, which matches `/<!DOCTYPE/i`) and carrying absolute server paths (`src/lib/db/result.ts:476`, `node_modules/@supabase/postgrest-js/dist/index.mjs:122`);
- (b) an intermediary **JSON envelope with no `message` field** crosses whole through `messageOf`'s `JSON.stringify` — `{"success":false,"errors":[…],"ray":"…"}` (114 chars) and `[{"message":"<html>nope</html>"}]`, which nests a document inside a part that does not begin with `<`;
- (c) pre-existing and **reachable today**, also in DEBT-0006's before-evidence: a transport failure puts runtime frames on the card — `pending_claims — TypeError: fetch failed / Caused by: Error: getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND db.invalid / at GetAddrInfoReqWrap.onlookupall [as oncomplete] (node:dns:121:26)`.

**Ruled in scope, and ruled as a WIDENING of the one derivation rather than three fixes** (architect, 2026-09-11). BUG-0170 decided the right thing in the right place; what it anchored on — "the first non-blank character is `<`, nothing else inspected" — turns out to be a hole of its own shape: (a) and (b) are the same foreign text arriving one field over or one quote deep, and (a) re-matches the very regex that ticket's pin asserts against. Three residuals of one class, all reachable from one function, is the definition of a class this app decides once. What it is NOT: a second reduction beside a surface (nothing outside `result.ts` gains a line), and not a scrub — see the refusals below.

**The class, stated positively:** an account carries the parts the DATABASE authored. A part the CLIENT authored ABOUT a failure — a runtime's stack frames, a body we had to serialise because it carried no message — is not the database's words, and is answered the way BUG-0170 answered a document: **counted, never quoted.**

**Three anchored questions, asked of each part in `errorMessage`'s loop, and no fourth:**

1. **Did WE serialise it?** `messageOf` fell to `JSON.stringify` because the value carried no string `message`. That is provenance, not text: the question inspects nothing at all. Such a part is replaced by an app-authored clause naming its character count. (Answers (b), nested document and all.)
2. **Is it a DOCUMENT?** first non-blank character `<` — unchanged, already shipped.
3. **Does it carry RUNTIME FRAMES?** a line whose text after leading whitespace begins `at ` and which ends in `)` or in `:<digits>:<digits>` — the V8 frame format, a specified format rather than a family of adversary text. Those LINES are dropped; every non-frame line of the part is kept verbatim and re-asked questions 1-2. (Answers (a) and (c).)

**What is NOT inspected, and never becomes a check here** (LESSONS 4, ARCHITECTURE §7 common violations row 15 — the blocklist-chased-one-family-at-a-time class): no scanning of prose for a document fragment ANYWHERE inside it, no entity decoding, no tag stripping, no length cap on prose, no codepoint rules, and no matching on a runtime's vocabulary (`SyntaxError`, `is not valid JSON`, `ENOTFOUND`). After question 3, (a)'s account is the runtime's own one-line diagnostic, an 11-character quotation of the body included; that is the runtime's sentence about what it read, it is bounded, and chasing it would cost the detector this ticket exists to refuse.

**This REVERSES one line of admin-window/BUG-0016, deliberately, and not silently.** That ticket's residual was pinned as "carries the client's whole account of a transport failure, untrimmed … stack frame included; trimming it silently is the defect" (`tests/offline/dashboard/page.test.ts:1183`, `tests/offline/queues/page.test.ts:922`). What BUG-0016 was actually protecting is the CAUSE — `message` alone said `TypeError: fetch failed` and named nothing, and the cause lives in `details` AFTER the first frame line. So the cause still crosses whole (dropping frame LINES, never truncating at the first one, is the whole reason the rule is written that way), and the trim is not silent: the part says how many frames were dropped. Those two cases are rewritten to assert the cause and the frame count instead of `makeNetworkError` — rewritten, never deleted. `tests/fixtures/stub-client.ts`'s `transportFailure()` is used by ten suites and is NOT rewritten; new shapes are local literals in `result.test.ts`, as that file already does.

**Scope and landing.** `src/lib/db/result.ts` + `tests/offline/db/result.test.ts` are the fix; `tests/offline/dashboard/page.test.ts` and `tests/offline/queues/page.test.ts` are in scope because they PIN the behaviour this ticket changes (pre-scoped by grepping `makeNetworkError` through the test tree — those two files and the fixture are the only hits). No dependency edge: BUG-0172 writes `components/ui/paging.tsx` and `components/ui/window-line.tsx` (disjoint), DEBT-0017 writes `lib/paging/machine.ts` (disjoint), TASK-0070..0073 and TASK-0077 name none of these four files. If TASK-0074 is ever dispatched while this is open, this one lands first — the same ruling BUG-0170 carried.

## Acceptance criteria
ONE DERIVATION, WIDENED IN PLACE. The decision of what an account may carry stays inside errorMessage's part assembly in src/lib/db/result.ts — the function every kind:"error" result already passes through — and grows from one question to three, asked of each part in order: (1) did messageOf SERIALISE this value because it carried no string message (provenance, no text inspected); (2) is its first non-blank character "<" (unchanged); (3) does it carry RUNTIME FRAMES — a line whose text after leading whitespace begins "at " and ends in ")" or in ":<digits>:<digits>". No fourth question is added, no surface below gains a reduction of its own (src/lib/paging/machine.ts and src/components/ui/error-line.tsx are unchanged by this ticket), and every other read in lib/db/** inherits the widening with no line of its own.

COUNTED, NEVER QUOTED — the two REPLACING answers. A part that answers (1) or (2) is replaced by an app-authored clause that quotes no part of what arrived and states its real character count, exactly as the shipped document clause does: for the 4,547-character Cloudflare page the account still contains 4547 (or 4,547) and matches neither /<!DOCTYPE/i nor /<\/?html[\s>]/i, and for a serialised envelope of N characters the account contains N and contains neither the envelope's keys nor its values — including when the envelope nests a document (the measured [{"message":"<html>nope</html>"}] and {"success":false,"errors":[...],"ray":"..."}). Neither clause is one of this app's generic sentences ("something went wrong", "sorry"), neither is blank and neither is a bare number: an operator can tell that something other than the database answered.

FRAMES ARE DROPPED, THE CAUSE IS NOT, AND THE DROP IS NOT SILENT. A part answering (3) keeps EVERY non-frame line verbatim, joined as one line, and drops only the frame lines — never truncated at the first frame, because postgrest-js puts the real cause AFTER it (admin-window/BUG-0016: reading message alone shipped the bare wrapper, and that must not come back). The kept lines are re-asked questions 1-2, so a header that is itself a document is still counted. The part then states how many frames were dropped, in the app's own words with the number in it — so what BUG-0016 called "trimming it silently" is not what happens here. On QA's measured transport failure the account names the object, says "fetch failed", says "getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND db.invalid", states a frame count, and contains no "node:dns:", no "at GetAddrInfoReqWrap" and no absolute path; on QA's measured 2xx-non-JSON failure the account is under 200 characters, contains no path under node_modules/ or src/, and still carries the runtime's one-line diagnostic.

NOT A SCRUB, AND THE REFUSALS ARE PART OF THE BAR. Nothing in src/lib/db/result.ts inspects prose for a document fragment ANYWHERE inside it, decodes entities, strips tags, caps the length of prose, filters codepoints, or matches a runtime's vocabulary — the strings "DOCTYPE", "SyntaxError", "not valid JSON" and "ENOTFOUND" appear on no CODE line of that file (a docstring explaining the class may name them; the check reads code lines only, ARCHITECTURE §10). canSpellUrlValue in src/lib/url/spellable.ts is still neither called nor named here: it answers the URL-value question, and a shared predicate answering a second question is widened by whichever question broke last (LESSONS 4).

THE FIXTURE TABLE (LESSONS 8 — every anchor proved on one input it MUST reduce and one it must NOT). REDUCED, in tests/offline/db/result.test.ts, each a local literal: (1) QA's 697-802-character SyntaxError-with-stack whose details is err.stack and whose message quotes "<!DOCTYPE "; (2) the measured DNS transport failure with a "Caused by:" line and a "    at GetAddrInfoReqWrap.onlookupall (node:dns:121:26)" frame; (3) the 114-character no-message JSON envelope; (4) the array envelope nesting <html>; (5) the three document shapes and the JWT-carrying document BUG-0170 already pins. CROSSING WHOLE, unchanged: (6) the four-field PostgREST refusal, code still in parentheses; (7) "operator does not exist: text <-> integer" with code 42883 on a TABLE read, angle-bracket operator included — the fixture that stops this from becoming a bracket scrub; (8) a duplicate-key message quoting a Hangul value, never reduced, truncated or transliterated; (9) a database message containing an indented line that begins "at " but is NOT a frame (it ends in neither ")" nor ":<digits>:<digits>") — the fixture that stops question 3 from becoming a prose filter; (10) an app-thrown Error whose message is "SUPABASE_URL is not set", which crosses verbatim (STACK.md §5's launch diagnosis depends on that sentence reaching the panel).

EVERYTHING ELSE IN THE SUITE IS GREEN WITH NO CASE REWRITTEN except the two this ticket's own ruling reverses. BUG-0170's pin — it("does not carry an intermediary's HTML page into the account (admin-window/BUG-0170)") — keeps its name, stays a plain it, and is extended rather than duplicated; the cause/wrapper/order/cycle/redaction/classification cases of tests/offline/db/result.test.ts pass unchanged, including "carries the cause out of details when the message is only a wrapper", "says the wrapper once, not twice", "carries message, details, hint and code, in that order", "carries the message of a thrown Error, a thrown string, and a bare object" and "invents nothing when the client said nothing at all". The two REWRITTEN cases are tests/offline/dashboard/page.test.ts and tests/offline/queues/page.test.ts, "carries the client's whole account of a transport failure, untrimmed": each keeps a case about a transport failure, asserts the CAUSE and the frame count reach the rendered card, and no longer asserts "makeNetworkError" does. tests/fixtures/stub-client.ts is not edited. npm test, npm run lint and the targeted offline suites are green on the landed tree.

THE TWO NEW CASES ARE NAMED, so the bar is machine-checkable in the presence form: it("drops the runtime frames of a stack, keeping the cause and saying how many went") and it("counts a body it had to serialise, and nothing of what it said") in tests/offline/db/result.test.ts. Both are plain it, neither is an it.fails, and each carries its reduced fixture AND its must-not-touch twin from the table above.
