# The entity picker manages no focus at all, and its hint promises an Escape that only works while focus is inside the panel

## Description
M2 structure walk finding (architect, 2026-09-09), measured on run/admin-window.

The picker and `EditableCell` are the app's two inline-edit widgets and BUG-0111/0119 deliberately made them share their status clock and the page's one refusal slot. They do not share the third thing an edit widget owns: where focus is.

**The measurement.** `grep -c '\.focus()'`: `src/components/EditableCell.tsx` = 2, `src/components/records/entity-picker.tsx` = 0. The cell's own docstring (:49) states the contract it keeps — 'an edit the operator ends with Enter or Escape puts focus back on that button'. The picker never moves focus into the panel it opens and never restores it to the Choose button when the panel closes or a choice is refused.

**The half-promise.** `PICKER_HINT` (:153) is shown where the operator is about to choose and says an Escape cancels, without qualification. The Escape handler (`:643-656`) is bound to the OPEN PANEL's `onKeyDown`, so it fires only while focus is inside the panel. BUG-0119's fix comment (:536) records the state this leaves: 'a refusal leaves the panel open with focus wherever the operator put it'. After a refusal, an Escape from outside the panel retires the red line (the page-wide rule BUG-0119 armed) and leaves the panel open — two different meanings for one key, from one sentence that promises one.

LOOK_AND_FEEL bar 9: 'Every action on every page is reachable by Tab with a visible focus ring, and no verdict, sort, or edit is available only on hover.' A panel that opens without receiving focus is reachable only by tabbing forward through however many rows the search returned, and a panel that closes without returning focus drops the operator at the top of the document.

**What is NOT in this ticket.** The wording of `PICKER_HINT` is the designer's jurisdiction, not a criterion here — the ticket fixes the behaviour so that whatever the hint says can be true, and the walk grades the sentence.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Opening the picker moves keyboard focus into the panel, at the place the operator will act (the search field). Closing it — by the Choose button, by a choice that lands, or by Escape — returns focus to the Choose button, the contract `EditableCell` already keeps.
2. Escape means one thing wherever focus is while the panel is open: it closes the panel. An Escape pressed after a refusal, from inside or outside the panel, produces the same outcome, so the hint beside the control cannot be half-true. A refusal still retires through the page's one refusal slot and `retiresRefusal`'s single answer — BUG-0119's ruling is not reopened, and a retirement still does not close the panel on its own.
3. Focus is never dropped to the document: after a refusal, after a save that lands, and after a save that is superseded by another widget, focus is on a real focusable element of this widget.
4. Look and feel (LOOK_AND_FEEL, 'Inputs and inline edit' and bar 9): every focusable element of the picker shows the 2px accent outline at 1px offset on keyboard focus, with no `outline: none` anywhere in the widget; the whole choose-search-pick-confirm path is completable by Tab and Enter alone, with no pointer. The panel's focus ring and both themes at every supported width are (human-checkable) at the walk.
5. Every case BUG-0097 (a second click during an in-flight save), BUG-0102 (the picker live during a settlement), BUG-0111 (the confirmation clock) and BUG-0119 (the refusal's lifetime and the page's one slot) pinned still holds — this ticket adds focus, it does not re-decide any of them.
6. `npm run lint` and `tsc --noEmit` clean.
