# The paging control is the only full-width button in the app — 1216px with a centred label, where every other button is intrinsic width

## Description
Walked on a production build against staging (own port 8795, my PID, killed; reads only), 1440x900 and 1280x900.

MEASURED. The "Show the next 50 claims" / "Show the next 50 events" control is 1216px wide at 1440 (1056px at 1280) — the full content column — with its 12px label centred in it. Every other button in this app renders at its intrinsic width: on /records/walk_sandbox/…0001 the five controls measure 120, 193, 15, 41 and 74px. The paging control is the only full-bleed button in the product.

WHY IT IS A VIOLATION. LOOK_AND_FEEL's Look exists so that "two builders who never meet produce the same-looking app", and the brownfield stance pins the language as "a dense, square-cornered, mono-heavy operator console". The Buttons rule spells a button as "body sans, 4px radius, 6/12 padding … secondary: 1px hairline border, transparent fill, primary text" — a 6px/12px padding rule applied to a box stretched to 1216px produces something that is not this app's button. At that size a hairline-bordered, centre-labelled slab reads as a banner or a container ("Structure comes from rules and fills. There is no elevation in this app"), and it is by area the loudest control on either page while being, correctly, a SECONDARY one. Bar: "One primary action per screen" — this is not the primary action on /claims or /browse, and it out-shouts everything that is.

MECHANISM (named as evidence, not as a prescription): PageMore's wrapper is a flex column, whose default align-items: stretch pulls the button to the container width. Any of self-start / w-fit / items-start fixes it; the choice is the builder's.

Traces to VISION: "Ben, the ecosystem's operator, at breakfast … a fluent operator, not a newcomer" — the operator reads this console by shape, and a control that does not look like the app's other controls costs that reading.

NOT IN SCOPE: the label (a verb plus its object, correct per copy bar 1), the button's ink, border, radius, padding, type step and disabled treatment (all measured correct: #1e2939 on transparent, 1px #d1d5dc, 4px radius, 6px/12px padding, 12/18 sans, 50% opacity + not-allowed when loading, accent 2px/1px-offset focus ring), and the placement of the refusal above it.

Evidence: agenticflow/tracker/evidence/M3/designer/browse-refusal-nonjson.png (the control at full width under the table), browse-paged-1280.png. Gitignored; the widths above are the durable record.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The paging control is sized by its content, not by its container: asserted on RENDERED MARKUP offline, through the same driver the other paging states are graded through — the control's own box is not stretched to the column it sits in, and the wrapper that holds the control, the refusal and the two end-of-the-road sentences imposes no full-width stretch on the button. (The measured widths are the walk's half of this bar — criterion 6.)
2. It sits at the start of the content column (left-aligned with the table's left edge), and the refusal line and the two end-of-the-road sentences keep their current placement and full-width wrapping.
3. Its ink, border, radius, padding, type step, disabled treatment and focus ring are unchanged — 1px hairline border, transparent fill, primary text, 4px radius, 6px/12px padding, body sans, 50% opacity and not-allowed while loading, 2px accent outline at 1px offset on keyboard focus. The Button primitive itself is not restyled.
4. Both end-of-the-road sentences ([data-paging="exhausted"] and [data-paging="limit"]) still render as text, not as a button-shaped box, and no arm gains or loses an element.
5. No other button in the app changes width: the record page's controls and every other Button call site render byte-identical markup.
6. (human-checkable) On a production build at 1440x900 and at 1280x900, the [data-paging] button measures under 260px and does not grow when the content column does; read beside the record page's controls it must be the same component — an intrinsic-width secondary button in a dense console, not a full-column slab.
7. Lint is clean and a PRODUCTION build is green.
