# The Dashboard's attention cards put a chip inside the link: the severity badge dissolves into the card under the pointer

## Description
The second instance of the rule admin-window/BUG-0113 earned. LOOK_AND_FEEL, "Chips and badges": **"A badge never sits inside a link, and a link never wears one … any badge classifying it sits beside it, never around it. Walkable: no anchor inside `main` contains a chip-filled span."**

**Expected**: no anchor inside `main` contains a chip-filled span, on any page.

**Found**, on `/` (Dashboard): both attention `StatCard`s are anchors (`src/components/ui/stat-card.tsx:82` — `href` makes the whole card an `<a>`), and their sub-line renders the max severity as a `<Badge>` INSIDE that anchor (`src/app/page.tsx:193`, `AttentionDetail`). Delivered markup, from the running build:

```html
<a href="/queues?kind=signal" class="flex flex-col gap-1 border border-hairline bg-surface p-3 transition-colors hover:bg-chrome">
  …<span class="type-data inline-block rounded-control bg-chrome px-2 py-0.5 text-attention">high</span>…
</a>
```

**And it costs the reader something the /claims instance did not.** The anchor carries `hover:bg-chrome`; the Badge carries `bg-chrome`. Same token, so under the pointer the chip's fill and the card's fill become the same colour and the chip's box disappears — the one chip on the Dashboard that carries severity colour stops reading as a chip exactly when the reader is pointing at it.

Measured in bundled Chromium on a `next build` of run/admin-window at 23ea107, served on 127.0.0.1:8905 against staging, viewport 1440x900, mouse parked at (0,0) then hovering `main a[href='/queues?kind=signal']`, 400ms settle. Crop = the chip's bounding box + 6/4px margin (54x28):

| theme | state | card `background-color` | chip `background-color` | card-fill px in crop | chip-fill px in crop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| light | at rest | `rgb(255, 255, 255)` | `rgb(249, 250, 251)` | 676 | 691 |
| light | hovered | `rgb(249, 250, 251)` | `rgb(249, 250, 251)` | **0** | 1383 |
| dark | at rest | `rgb(16, 24, 40)` | `rgb(30, 41, 57)` | 676 | 683 |
| dark | hovered | `rgb(30, 41, 57)` | `rgb(30, 41, 57)` | **0** | 1359 |

Zero card-fill pixels in the crop means the crop is one flat colour: the chip's box is gone. Raw numbers and the script: `agenticflow/tracker/evidence/BUG-0113/probe3.json`, `probe3.py`; the whole-app walkable-form sweep that found it: `qa-walk.py`, `qa-walk.json` (`/`, `/browse`, `/claims`, `/claims?tab=standing`, `/cycles`, `/queues`, `/queues/<id>`, `/sources`, `/records/<table>/<id>`, both themes — `/` is the ONLY page with a chip-filled span inside an anchor; `/claims` is 0/74 anchors since BUG-0113 landed).

**Repro**
1. `next build && next start` on a free port with staging credentials; sign in with a minted walker cookie (STACK.md §"the production-like walk").
2. Open `/` at 1440x900. `getComputedStyle(document.querySelector("main a[href='/queues?kind=signal'] span.rounded-control")).backgroundColor` -> `rgb(249, 250, 251)`.
3. Hover the card. `getComputedStyle(document.querySelector("main a[href='/queues?kind=signal']")).backgroundColor` -> `rgb(249, 250, 251)` — the same value. The chip is now invisible against the card.

**Note for the fix — this is the designer's call, not a builder's pick.** The two candidate shapes are (a) move the severity badge OUT of the anchor so it sits beside the card's link body as the ruling prescribes, or (b) stop making the whole card an anchor and put the app's one link spelling on the words. (b) touches every StatCard on the Dashboard and is a bigger change than this ticket; (a) is local to `AttentionDetail`. A third question the designer may want to settle at the same time: a `StatCard` with `href` announces itself as a link ONLY under the pointer (`color: rgb(30, 41, 57)`, `text-decoration-line: none` at rest, `hover:bg-chrome` its only mark) — that is the bar-10 shape BUG-0054/BUG-0099 removed everywhere else, and it is what makes the badge harmless-looking here. Not in this ticket's scope; named so it is not rediscovered.

**Pin.** `tests/offline/dashboard/page.test.ts`, "never draws a chip inside a link, on the card the severity sits in" — a strict `it.fails` pin (it XPASSes red the day this is fixed). It asserts through `chipsInsideLinks` in `tests/fixtures/link-spelling.ts`, which derives the chip's classes by RENDERING `<Badge>` rather than repeating any class literal, so a restyle of the chip moves the guard with it. Watched red before pinning, with the marker flipped to a plain `it`:

```
expected [ …(2) ] to deeply equal []
+ [
+   "Open decisions2higholdest 10d ago — type-data inline-block rounded-control bg-chrome px-2 py-0.5 text-attention",
+   "Open signals1higholdest 12d ago — type-data inline-block rounded-control bg-chrome px-2 py-0.5 text-attention",
+ ]
```

The helper's own non-vacuity is proved on both fixtures in `tests/offline/ui/link-spelling.test.ts` ("the rule that a badge never sits inside a link"): it flags a chip inside the anchor and stays green when the chip sits beside it, which is the shape the ruling prescribes.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **No anchor in the delivered markup of any page contains a chip-filled span** (LOOK_AND_FEEL, *Chips and badges*; ARCHITECTURE.md §7, promoted to a rule at this second instance on 2026-09-09). On `/` the two attention `StatCard`s are the open instance. Measured structurally by `chipsInsideLinks` (`tests/fixtures/link-spelling.ts`), whose chip classes are derived by RENDERING `<Badge>`, so no class literal is pinned anywhere.
2. **The repo-wide guard lands with this fix** — Common violations row 13 said "if a second instance appears, promote both", and this is the second instance. The assertion goes in `tests/offline/ui/link-spelling.test.ts`, the one owner of link spelling, which already proves `chipsInsideLinks` non-vacuous on two fixtures. It sweeps **every route the filesystem offers**, not the Dashboard alone: drive `loadSurfaces()` / `pageRoutes()` from `tests/offline/absence/surfaces.ts` — the shared harness `absence/pages.test.ts`, `blank-cells.test.ts` and `in-window.test.ts` already drive — against `populatedScript(surface)`, and assert `chipsInsideLinks($, $.root())` is `[]` on each. Copy only the file-scoped `vi.mock("@/lib/db/client")` + `scriptDatabase` stanza those three files each carry (a `vi.mock` is hoisted per file and cannot be imported); copy no walk and no helper. Assert the sweep covers exactly the routes `pageRoutes()` found, so a page added later inherits the rule rather than a comment about it. **Measured by the architect on the pre-fix tree, 2026-09-09**: the sweep returns 2 hits, both on `/`, and 0 on the other seven routes — that is this ticket's red-to-green target and the proof the guard cannot pass vacuously. Its known limit, which the walk still owns and which the test should say out loud: it sees only the states `populatedScript` renders.
3. **The fix shape, ruled by the architect on 2026-09-09 — the chip goes, the word stays.** `AttentionDetail` (`src/app/page.tsx:193`) renders the max severity as the registry's word verbatim with NO chip: no fill (`bg-*`), no `rounded-control`, no inline-block padding box — only the severity's own ink. This is the designer's BUG-0113 precedent applied (there, too, the chip was dropped and the word kept), and it removes the defect at its root: with no fill on the word, there is no fill that can become the card's fill under the pointer. The severity-to-ink mapping stays spelled ONCE — `TONE` in `src/components/ui/badge.tsx` — so export it and consume it; `src/app/page.tsx` does not repeat a class literal or grow a second copy of "what colour is a severity". `Badge`'s own rendering is unchanged.
4. **Out of bounds for this fix.** (i) Restructuring `StatCard` so the card stops being one anchor, or so the anchor wraps only part of the card: that is the bar-10 question this bug's own note parked (a card-shaped link announcing itself only under the pointer), it changes the hover affordance of every `href` card, and it is a designer's ruling to ask for, not a builder's pick. (ii) A link-flavoured or interactive `Badge` variant — the designer rejected it on BUG-0113 twice over: a badge is not interactive, and a second link spelling is a second answer to "does this text go somewhere". (iii) Giving the severity word the link's ink or the underline: it is a value, not a link, and must satisfy `expectNotDrawnAsLink` (`tests/fixtures/link-spelling.ts`). (iv) Every badge that is NOT inside an anchor stays exactly as it is — `claim-list.tsx`'s bucket badge, the review header's five, the tables' — the rule is about anchors, not about badges.
5. **Browser measurement, replacing the old criterion 1's fill-against-fill pair, which the ruled fix makes unsatisfiable** (with no chip there is no chip fill to distinguish from the card's): on `/` at 1440x900, in both themes, at rest AND hovered, the severity word renders in the severity ink over the card's fill and stays legible in all four states — ink pixels present in the word's crop every time, and the word never becomes the fill it sits on. The card's own hover fill is unchanged (`bg-chrome`), and the card still navigates.
6. The severity word the card shows is unchanged: the registry's word verbatim, no number and no percentage beside it, and the oldest-open age with its absolute instant in the `title` still render on the same card. Whatever `data-` hook the Dashboard suite reads that severity by survives the change. Both counts stay linked to their kind's queue (`/queues?kind=decision`, `/queues?kind=signal`); no card is added or removed and the two-column grid is unchanged.
7. `tests/offline/dashboard/page.test.ts`'s pin "never draws a chip inside a link, on the card the severity sits in" is a plain `it(...)` and green — the `it.fails` marker is removed as part of the fix, never left XPASSing, and no other `it.fails` is introduced in that file.
8. `/claims` is untouched — the bucket links keep the spelling admin-window/BUG-0113 bought (`tests/offline/claims/page.test.ts` green, including its own `chipsInsideLinks` assertion).
9. `npm run lint` and `tsc --noEmit` clean; the targeted offline files green. The targeted set is enough even though `badge.tsx` is a shared primitive: the change there is an export, not a rendering change (criterion 3 pins that), and the new whole-window sweep renders every page in this ticket's own check.
