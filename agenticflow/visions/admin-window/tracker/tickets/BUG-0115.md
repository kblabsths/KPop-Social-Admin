# The Dashboard's attention cards put a chip inside the link: the severity badge dissolves into the card under the pointer

## Description
The second instance of the rule admin-window/BUG-0113 earned. LOOK_AND_FEEL, "Chips and badges": **"A badge never sits inside a link, and a link never wears one … any badge classifying it sits beside it, never around it. Walkable: no anchor inside `main` contains a chip-filled span."**

**Expected**: no anchor inside `main` contains a chip-filled span, on any page.

**Found**, on `/` (Dashboard): both attention `StatCard`s are anchors (`src/components/ui/stat-card.tsx:82` — `href` makes the whole card an `<a>`), and their sub-line renders the max severity as a `<Badge>` INSIDE that anchor (`src/app/page.tsx:193`, `AttentionDetail`). Delivered markup, from the running build:

```html
<a href="/queues?kind=signal" class="flex flex-col gap-1 border border-hairline bg-surface p-3 transition-colors hover:bg-chrome">
  …<span class="type-data inline-block rounded-control bg-chrome px-2 py-0.5 text-attention">high</span>…
</a>
```

**And it costs the reader something the /claims instance did not.** The anchor carries `hover:bg-chrome`; the Badge carries `bg-chrome`. Same token, so under the pointer the chip's fill and the card's fill become the same colour and the chip's box disappears — the one chip on the Dashboard that carries severity colour stops reading as a chip exactly when the reader is pointing at it.

Measured in bundled Chromium on a `next build` of run/admin-window at 23ea107, served on 127.0.0.1:8905 against staging, viewport 1440x900, mouse parked at (0,0) then hovering `main a[href='/queues?kind=signal']`, 400ms settle. Crop = the chip's bounding box + 6/4px margin (54x28):

| theme | state | card `background-color` | chip `background-color` | card-fill px in crop | chip-fill px in crop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| light | at rest | `rgb(255, 255, 255)` | `rgb(249, 250, 251)` | 676 | 691 |
| light | hovered | `rgb(249, 250, 251)` | `rgb(249, 250, 251)` | **0** | 1383 |
| dark | at rest | `rgb(16, 24, 40)` | `rgb(30, 41, 57)` | 676 | 683 |
| dark | hovered | `rgb(30, 41, 57)` | `rgb(30, 41, 57)` | **0** | 1359 |

Zero card-fill pixels in the crop means the crop is one flat colour: the chip's box is gone. Raw numbers and the script: `agenticflow/tracker/evidence/BUG-0113/probe3.json`, `probe3.py`; the whole-app walkable-form sweep that found it: `qa-walk.py`, `qa-walk.json` (`/`, `/browse`, `/claims`, `/claims?tab=standing`, `/cycles`, `/queues`, `/queues/<id>`, `/sources`, `/records/<table>/<id>`, both themes — `/` is the ONLY page with a chip-filled span inside an anchor; `/claims` is 0/74 anchors since BUG-0113 landed).

**Repro**
1. `next build && next start` on a free port with staging credentials; sign in with a minted walker cookie (STACK.md §"the production-like walk").
2. Open `/` at 1440x900. `getComputedStyle(document.querySelector("main a[href='/queues?kind=signal'] span.rounded-control")).backgroundColor` -> `rgb(249, 250, 251)`.
3. Hover the card. `getComputedStyle(document.querySelector("main a[href='/queues?kind=signal']")).backgroundColor` -> `rgb(249, 250, 251)` — the same value. The chip is now invisible against the card.

**Note for the fix — this is the designer's call, not a builder's pick.** The two candidate shapes are (a) move the severity badge OUT of the anchor so it sits beside the card's link body as the ruling prescribes, or (b) stop making the whole card an anchor and put the app's one link spelling on the words. (b) touches every StatCard on the Dashboard and is a bigger change than this ticket; (a) is local to `AttentionDetail`. A third question the designer may want to settle at the same time: a `StatCard` with `href` announces itself as a link ONLY under the pointer (`color: rgb(30, 41, 57)`, `text-decoration-line: none` at rest, `hover:bg-chrome` its only mark) — that is the bar-10 shape BUG-0054/BUG-0099 removed everywhere else, and it is what makes the badge harmless-looking here. Not in this ticket's scope; named so it is not rediscovered.

**Pin.** `tests/offline/dashboard/page.test.ts`, "never draws a chip inside a link, on the card the severity sits in" — a strict `it.fails` pin (it XPASSes red the day this is fixed). It asserts through `chipsInsideLinks` in `tests/fixtures/link-spelling.ts`, which derives the chip's classes by RENDERING `<Badge>` rather than repeating any class literal, so a restyle of the chip moves the guard with it. Watched red before pinning, with the marker flipped to a plain `it`:

```
expected [ …(2) ] to deeply equal []
+ [
+   "Open decisions2higholdest 10d ago — type-data inline-block rounded-control bg-chrome px-2 py-0.5 text-attention",
+   "Open signals1higholdest 12d ago — type-data inline-block rounded-control bg-chrome px-2 py-0.5 text-attention",
+ ]
```

The helper's own non-vacuity is proved on both fixtures in `tests/offline/ui/link-spelling.test.ts` ("the rule that a badge never sits inside a link"): it flags a chip inside the anchor and stays green when the chip sits beside it, which is the shape the ruling prescribes.

## Acceptance criteria
1. No anchor inside `main` contains a chip-filled span, on `/`. Measured on the delivered markup by `chipsInsideLinks` and, in the browser, by the pair the defect was measured with: hovering the attention card leaves the severity chip's own fill distinguishable from the card's — the chip's crop holds BOTH fills at rest AND hovered, in both themes, at 1440x900.
2. `tests/offline/dashboard/page.test.ts`'s pin "never draws a chip inside a link, on the card the severity sits in" is a plain `it(...)` and green — the `it.fails` marker is removed as part of the fix, never left XPASSing.
3. The severity word the card shows is unchanged: the registry's word verbatim, no number and no percentage beside it, and the oldest-open age with its absolute instant in the `title` still render on the same card. Both counts stay linked to their kind's queue (`/queues?kind=decision`, `/queues?kind=signal`); no card is added or removed and the two-column grid is unchanged.
4. `/claims` is untouched — the bucket links keep the spelling admin-window/BUG-0113 bought (`tests/offline/claims/page.test.ts` green, including its own `chipsInsideLinks` assertion).
5. `npm run lint` and `tsc --noEmit` clean; the offline suite green.
