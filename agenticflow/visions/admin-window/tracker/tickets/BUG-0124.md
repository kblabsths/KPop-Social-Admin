# The review item's three counts state no relationship, and the lede claims a completeness the fold count contradicts

## Description
Filed from both M2 user-sim walks (2026-09-09). Two strangers, independently, tried to reconcile the three counts on the one open review item and could not.

**Devin** (`M2-usersim-devin.md` §2): "Three counts, three different numbers, no relationship stated … I *think* 700 is folds, 769 is claims in a 14-day window …, and 91 is the evidence rows actually listed. But I had to reconstruct that, and '91 of 91 resolved' reads like completeness right next to a headline of 700. **If I'd been asked to summarise this signal in one line for someone, I'd have got the number wrong.**"

**Priya** (`M2-usersim-priya.md` §2): "the header says 'asked again ×700' … the table shows 91 rows and claims to be complete — 'Every record folded into this signal is listed here'. Two numbers that look like they should agree, don't, and nothing explains the difference."

**Expected** — LOOK_AND_FEEL Voice bar 6 ("counts carry their noun") and bar 13's principle, which is this app's signature and the reason both sims trusted every other number on the screen: a number states what it counts and over what read. A screen may not assert a completeness its own read cannot support.

**Found**, measured on the walk instance (127.0.0.1:8771, staging, 2026-09-09), item `01a06287-5b67-7d1f-b4a5-d90aa93cb01a`, top to bottom:

| where | string on screen | population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| header | `asked again ×700` (`src/components/review/item-header.tsx:95-97`, `review_items.folded_count`) | folds |
| lede | "One source, many records stuck the same way. **Every record folded into this signal is listed here**" (`src/components/review/shape-views.tsx:402-406`) | claims a total |
| table | 91 rows | the item's evidence ids, resolved |
| dial | `stuck records 769` — "stuck records this source has in the window below" | a different read, and **this one states its scope**, correctly |
| footer | "91 of 91 evidence ids resolved to a claim, in the order they folded in." (`src/app/queues/[reviewItemId]/page.tsx:432`) | the same 91 |

The header's 700 and the table's 91 are never related. `accountingOf` already handles the *other* direction — when the evidence array stores more ids than it holds distinct, it says "It stores N ids in all: a claim that folded in again is appended, and counts once here" — but here stored == distinct == 91, so nothing on the page mentions the 700 at all, and the lede asserts that those 91 rows ARE every folded record. Admin cannot know that: `folded_count` and the `evidence` array are two columns of the same row and this page compares them nowhere.

The dial is the model to copy: 769 with "stuck records this source has in the window below" printed under it. Neither of the other two counts has its equivalent.

Provenance checked before filing: `git log` on `item-header.tsx`, `shape-views.tsx`, `queues/[reviewItemId]/page.tsx` returns ticket-bearing commits only. Not a human-lane divergence.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **The fold count states what a fold is, and how many records the table below holds.** Wherever `folded_count` renders on the detail page, the screen states — in the app's voice, in the same block — that a fold is a re-ask and not a record, and names the number of records the evidence lists (`×700 folds over the 91 records below`, or equivalent). The two numbers appear near each other, both with their nouns.
2. **The lede claims no completeness the read cannot support.** "Every record folded into this signal is listed here" goes: the source-pattern lede says what the table holds — the claims behind the item's evidence ids — and says nothing about whether those account for every fold. Same register, no apology, no hedging word ("should", "roughly").
3. Where `folded_count` and the distinct evidence-id count DO agree, the page may say so plainly; where they do not, the page states both rather than choosing one.
4. The accounting sentence's existing arms are unchanged (`91 of 91 evidence ids resolved…`, and the stored-vs-distinct clause), and the dial keeps its own line verbatim.
5. `folded_count` is still rendered verbatim as the machine's number in mono; no computed ratio, no percentage, no severity-like score (VISION non-goal: "no severity formula").
6. Human-check (human-checkable): on the walk instance at 1440x900, a reader who has never seen this item can say, out loud and correctly, what each of `700`, `769` and `91` counts, without scrolling twice or opening another page.
7. `npm run lint` and `tsc --noEmit` clean; the offline suite green.
