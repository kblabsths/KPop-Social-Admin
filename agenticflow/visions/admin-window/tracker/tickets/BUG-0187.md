# A pretty-printed document whose text sits on its own lines is counted once per fragment, and its own text crosses verbatim into the account

## Description
**Module:** `src/lib/db/result.ts` — `askedLineByLine`, the per-line arm of question 2.

**Expected** (the standing property, admin-window/BUG-0170 and BUG-0173: "text this app did not author never sits inside a sentence this app wrote", and admin-window/BUG-0182's criterion 1: an account that COUNTED a document is shorter than what it counted): a pretty-printed page one line below the database's prose is COUNTED — one clause, no byte of the page in the account.

**Found:** a document line is a line whose first non-blank character is `<`, so a line carrying only the page's TEXT — a sentence, a Ray ID, an IP — is read as the DATABASE'S prose. It ENDS the run, it is kept VERBATIM, and the one document is counted once per fragment between its text nodes.

Measured on run/admin-window at ac48f40 (the landed BUG-0182), through `classify` and rendered through `StateOf` -> `ErrorLine` (`[data-state="error"]`, cheerio `.text()`, so an escaped leak cannot hide):

1. A Cloudflare interstitial (23 lines, 386 chars) with five of its lines carrying text rather than a tag, one line below the client's own prose — `details = "reference 8f3c1\n<page>"`: part **402 chars -> account 664 chars** (LONGER than the part it replaced), **6 document clauses**, and five runs of the intermediary's own characters reach the account and the rendered card verbatim: `Sorry, you have been blocked`, `You are unable to access this site`, `You can email the site owner to let them know you were blocked.`, `8f3c1de4b7c90a13` (the Ray ID) and `203.0.113.7` (an IP).
2. The SAME bytes as the WHOLE part: **1 clause, 80 chars, nothing leaked** — so the two granularities of question 2 answer 8.3x apart again on this shape, which is the divergence BUG-0182 closed for documents whose every line begins `<`.
3. Through the real `@supabase/supabase-js` client over a stubbed 400 transport, CRLF-separated: part 425 -> account 665, 6 clauses, the same five strings leaked, and into the PATCH route's JSON body (`Response.json({error: result.message})`, src/app/api/admin/records/[table]/[id]/route.ts:334).
4. Growth is linear in the page's text nodes, not bounded: 4 text nodes -> part 245 / account 527 (ratio 2.15, 5 clauses); 16 -> 851 / 1,793 (2.11, 17 clauses); 64 -> 3,299 / 6,881 (2.09, 65 clauses), with every one of the 64 text lines quoted verbatim.

**Reachability:** the operator-visible error card on any page whose read meets an intermediary (`/claims` has six HEAD count reads and three cards), and the PATCH route's JSON body. A WAF interstitial is served pretty-printed, and a long sentence, a Ray ID and an IP routinely sit on lines of their own between their tags.

**Not culpable to admin-window/BUG-0182**, which implemented the architect's ruling faithfully and verified green by QA on the landed tree (all 7 criteria, all 7 checks). That ruling is "a RUN OF CONSECUTIVE DOCUMENT LINES is one document... the same `isDocument` predicate decides each line exactly as today, nothing new is inspected, no fourth question is added" — under which a text-node line is not a document line and the run must end there. Every document fixture in tests/offline/db/result.test.ts prints one element per line, so every line of them begins `<` and the shape was invisible to the ticket. The class is OLDER than BUG-0182 and was worse before it (the same part gave one clause PER LINE), so this is a residue of that fix, not a regression of it.

**The ruling this asks for, and why it is not a builder's choice.** The obvious shape — a line standing BETWEEN two document runs in one part belongs to the document — collides head-on with BUG-0182's criterion 3 over-grouping twin, `it("counts two documents in one part as two, not as one")`, which requires a line of the database's prose between two documents to stay the database's and to cross VERBATIM. Both cases are "a non-document line between two document runs" and the app cannot tell them apart with the one predicate it has. Whatever is chosen must not become a fourth question (no tag matching, no entity decoding, no length cap, no vocabulary — LESSONS 4, ARCHITECTURE §7 common violations row 15), and the whole-part arm must keep counting at the length of what arrived.

**Not admin-window/DEBT-0020** (the transport account's twice-stated cause sentence, chained behind on the same file): the repeated clauses here are all DIFFERENT strings, each carrying its own fragment's length, so an identical-line dedup collapses none of them.

**Repro (offline, exact):**
```

```
The pin is `it.fails("counts a pretty-printed document whose text sits on its own lines (admin-window/BUG-XXXX)")` in tests/offline/db/result.test.ts, watched RED as a plain `it` on ac48f40 (`AssertionError: quotes Sorry, you have been blocked: expected 'read failed reference 8f3c1 a 138-cha…' not to contain 'Sorry, you have been blocked'`) before it was flipped. **Flip it back to a plain `it` when this is fixed** — strict, so it reddens the day the divergence disappears. Its must-not-touch half is inside the same case and is GREEN today: the same bytes as the WHOLE part are one clause and leak nothing, which forbids the lazy fix of counting less.

## Acceptance criteria
THE RULING THE TICKET ASKS FOR (architect, 2026-09-11) — THE BAR, RESTATED ONCE, IN PLACE OF A FOURTH ANCHOR. **A part that carries a DOCUMENT anywhere is not the database's words, and is replaced WHOLE by ONE counted clause.** Question 2 is asked ONCE per part — never per line, never per run: does ANY line of the part, after its leading whitespace, begin `<`? If YES, the part is replaced by exactly one `documentInstead(raw)` clause, counted at the length of the part exactly as the client delivered it, and NOTHING else is said about that part: no line of it crosses, no frames clause, no second clause, whatever else it carried. If NO, the part is the database's words and is read exactly as it is today: runtime frames dropped and counted (question 3), every other line kept verbatim in order, and a part in which nothing answers returned byte-untouched.

WHY THIS ENDS THE CHAIN AND A FOURTH ANCHOR WOULD NOT (admin-window/BUG-0170 -> 0173 -> 0179 -> 0181 -> 0185 -> 0182 -> 0187 is ONE class: each ruling extended the derivation by one anchored question and the next QA lane found the shape it missed). Postgres does not speak markup. A part carrying markup was authored by something that does, and this app cannot attribute ANY line of such a part to the database — which this suite's own fixtures demonstrate: every prose line the landed rule lets cross beside a document is the intermediary's own (`reference 8f3c1`, a WAF reference id; `upstream said:`; `edge-cache-status: refused`). There is no fixture anywhere of a Postgres message sharing a part with markup, because there is no such shape. So the honest unit is the PART — which is also the unit ARCHITECTURE.md 4.1 was written in ("an account carries the PARTS the database authored ... a part that answers is replaced by an app-authored clause that COUNTS it and quotes none of it"). This ruling REMOVES machinery rather than adding it: question 2 stops being asked line by line, the run/offset bookkeeping admin-window/BUG-0182 added is deleted, and the two granularities that have answered differently on every shape since BUG-0179 collapse into ONE code path. `isDocument` keeps its body byte for byte; no tag matching, no entity decoding, no length cap, no vocabulary and no `includes("<")` appears anywhere on this path (LESSONS 4, ARCHITECTURE.md 7 Common violations row 15).

WHAT THE BAR DELIBERATELY DOES **NOT** SAY — decided here, so the next lane grades the bar instead of re-litigating it. (a) MULTI-LINE IS NOT FOREIGN: a database-authored message that spans lines still crosses WHOLE and verbatim (fixture 9, `indentedProse` — Postgres wraps its own DETAIL onto continuation lines, and counting such a part would replace a check-constraint message with a number, which is the opposite of "the app shows what the database said"). (b) FRAMES ARE NOT FOREIGN: a part carrying a V8 stack still keeps its non-frame lines (fixture 2 — `Caused by: Error: getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND db.invalid` is the cause admin-window/BUG-0016 exists to preserve). Frames stay a LINE question because V8's is a specified format, and they are asked only of parts that carry no document. (c) A DOCUMENT THAT DOES NOT OPEN A LINE — markup glued after prose on ONE line — is NOT caught and may not be: the only predicate that would (`part.includes("<")`) is refused by this suite's own fixture 7, `operator does not exist: text <-> integer`. Foreign PROSE carrying no markup at all is likewise outside question 2, and is answered where it can be by question 1 (provenance). (a)-(c) are DECIDED LIMITS, not missing anchors: a real body measured in shape (c) re-opens THIS BAR as a ruling; it never adds a fourth question.

1. THE LEAK IS CLOSED, AND GRADED AS THE BAR RATHER THAN AS A LIST OF STRINGS. On the shapes QA measured — the 23-line interstitial whose TEXT sits on its own lines one line below the client's prose, the 4/16/64-text-node ladder, and the same bytes CRLF-separated through the real `@supabase/supabase-js` client over a stubbed 400 transport — the account carries exactly ONE document clause and NO non-blank line of the part appears in it. Asserted generally (for every trimmed non-blank line of the part, the account does not contain it), so the next text node is covered by the same assertion, on the `DbResult` AND on the rendered card through `StateOf` -> `ErrorLine` read with cheerio `.text()` (an escaped leak cannot hide). The five strings QA named are among them: "Sorry, you have been blocked", "You are unable to access this site", "You can email the site owner to let them know you were blocked.", the Ray ID `8f3c1de4b7c90a13`, the IP `203.0.113.7`.

2. THE ACCOUNT IS SHORTER, AND BOUNDED BY THE CLAUSE INSTEAD OF BY THE PAGE. On each measured shape the account is SHORTER than the part it replaced, carries exactly one clause, and the clause's number is the length of the PART exactly as delivered (`raw.length`) — so QA's 402 -> one clause and 3,299 -> one clause, and growth is no longer linear in text nodes. Asserted as a property against the part (`account.length < part.length`, one clause counted through the suite's existing `clausesIn`), never against a recorded constant: the lengths QA recorded were the defect's. The property is asserted on the measured shapes, as in admin-window/BUG-0182 criterion 1 — a tiny document can still cost more to describe than it carried (a 72-character document costs ~80) and that is inherent to counting.

3. THE TWO RESIDUALS OF BUG-0182's CLOSE FOLD INTO THIS RULE, AND NEITHER BECOMES A TICKET. (i) A frame-SHAPED text line inside a document ("at least one parameter is required (see docs)" inside a pretty-printed `<Message>`, QA's part 128 -> account 219): the part carries a document, so it is ONE clause at the part's length, with NO "(1 runtime stack frame dropped)" and no split. (ii) A document with V8 frames appended (QA's 146-character document + two frames, which read "a 260-character markup document arrived"): ONE clause at the part's length, no frames clause — the SAME answer whether the document IS the whole part or sits below a line of it, which is the granularity disagreement QA recorded as residual (ii), now dissolved rather than patched. Both land as named cases in tests/offline/db/result.test.ts.

4. THE DERIVATION SHRINKS, INTO ONE NAMED HOME. src/lib/db/result.ts gains ONE predicate — `function carriesDocument(part: string): boolean`, "does any line of this part, trimmed, answer `isDocument`" (a runtime frame can never answer it: `isRuntimeFrame` requires the line to begin `at `). `partOfAccount` reads it in place of its whole-part `isDocument(trimmed)` arm and returns `documentInstead(raw)`, so `documentInstead` is CALLED FROM EXACTLY ONE PLACE — two occurrences of `documentInstead(` in the file, its definition and that call. `askedLineByLine` no longer asks question 2 at all: it drops and counts frames, keeps every other line verbatim, and returns the part untouched when no line is a frame — so the `PartLine` offsets and the run bookkeeping admin-window/BUG-0182 added are DELETED, not extended. `isDocument`, `isRuntimeFrame`, `documentInstead`, `framesInstead`, `serialisedInstead`, `databaseMessage`, `withoutSecrets` and `errorMessage`'s assembly keep their bodies and their wording byte for byte; `withoutSecrets` still runs LAST; `partOfAccount` still asks three questions in that order and gains no fourth.

5. WHAT STILL CROSSES — the must-not-touch half, non-vacuous (LESSONS 8), byte-identical accounts. Fixture 7 `operator does not exist: text <-> integer`; fixture 8's duplicate key with `Key (name)=(르세라핌) already exists.`; fixture 9 `indentedProse` (both lines, the newline and the four leading spaces, ending "(23514)"); fixture 2's transport failure (the cause sentence AND its frames clause); fixture 1's SyntaxError diagnostic with its eight frames counted; the four-field PostgREST refusal in field order ending "(42501)"; `new Error("SUPABASE_URL is not set")` exactly. The ten-fixture table keeps its five-and-five shape with every carries/never assertion unchanged and no case rewritten, and admin-window/BUG-0170's, BUG-0173's, BUG-0179's, BUG-0181's and BUG-0185's pins keep their NAMES (their stored checks grep them).

6. THE ASSERTIONS THIS RULING OVERTURNS, NAMED IN FULL — and nothing else landed moves. Each one blesses a foreign string crossing, which is why it goes; each gets a one-line comment naming this ticket. (a) `it("drops the runtime frames of a stack, keeping the cause and saying how many went")`, its document-inside block: `toContain("upstream said:")` becomes `not.toContain`, the asserted number becomes the PART's length, and `toMatch(/\b1\b[^)]{0,40}frame/)` becomes `not.toMatch(/frame/i)` (this is residual (ii)); its `indentedProse` must-not-touch block is unchanged. (b) `it("counts a document on any line of a part, framed or not")` (BUG-0179's pin, name kept): `toContain("reference 8f3c1")` becomes `not.toContain`, and `toContain(String(page.length))` becomes the part's length; its multi-line-prose must-not-touch block is unchanged. (c) `it("counts ONE document once, however many lines it was printed on (admin-window/BUG-0182)")` (name kept): the same single assertion. (d) `it("counts one document once on each shape QA measured")`: `toContain(shape.prose)` becomes `not.toContain`, and `documentClause(shape.document)` becomes `documentClause(shape.part)` — the helper still asks the APP for the clause and still retypes no word of it. (e) the over-grouping twin, per 7. (f) this ticket's own pin, per 8.

7. THE OVER-GROUPING TWIN IS SUPERSEDED, REWRITTEN RATHER THAN DELETED, AND STILL CARRIES BOTH DIRECTIONS (LESSONS 8). admin-window/BUG-0182 criterion 3 required two documents separated by a line of prose to count as TWO with that line crossing between them; under this bar a part carrying two documents is ONE FOREIGN PART. The case lands as `it("counts two documents in one part as two documents in ONE foreign part, no line quoted (admin-window/BUG-0187)")` — the name deliberately keeps the literal that admin-window/BUG-0182's stored check greps, so a closed ticket's evidence does not rot — and asserts: exactly ONE clause, at the WHOLE part's length; neither `upstream said:` nor the line standing between the two documents crosses; no `<`, no `AccessDenied`, no `Sorry, you have been blocked`, on the result and on the rendered card; and the account shorter than the part. Its MUST-NOT direction, which forbids the greedy rule, sits in the same case: a two-line DATABASE message carrying no document (fixture 9's shape) crosses byte-identical, both lines, indentation included.
