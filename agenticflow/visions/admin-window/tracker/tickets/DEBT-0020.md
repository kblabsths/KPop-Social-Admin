# The transport account states its cause sentence twice: postgrest-js appends cause.stack, whose head repeats the Caused-by line

## Description
**Module:** `src/lib/db/result.ts` — `errorMessage`'s part-level deduplication, which is a dedup BETWEEN parts and not WITHIN one.

**Found** (QA, at the close of admin-window/BUG-0173, residual (c); ruled cosmetic by the architect 2026-09-11): the transport account states its cause sentence twice. postgrest-js builds `details` for a fetch failure as the wrapper line, then a blank line, then `Caused by: <name>: <cause.message>`, and then — when the cause carries one — the cause's whole `stack`, whose FIRST line repeats `<name>: <cause.message>` verbatim (`node_modules/@supabase/postgrest-js/dist/index.mjs`, the `errorDetails += "\n" + cause.stack` arm). The frame lines go, both non-frame lines stay, and the account reads

    TypeError: fetch failed Caused by: Error: getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND db.invalid Error: getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND db.invalid (1 runtime stack frame dropped)

The existing dedup cannot catch it: `errorMessage` drops a PART another part already contains (which is how "says the wrapper once, not twice" stays green), and both copies here live inside ONE part, the reduced `details`.

**Why this is deferred and not folded into admin-window/BUG-0179** (architect ruling, 2026-09-11): the harm is one repeated ~40-character sentence the database itself authored — not an unbounded foreign payload, not a credential, nothing an intermediary controls. The fix is a line-level pass inside a part, and BUG-0179 is the ticket that makes the account's ONE derivation even; adding a fourth pass over prose in the same session is how the account grows the machinery LESSONS 4 exists to refuse. This lands afterwards, on the settled tree, or not at all.

**The trap the fix must avoid:** dropping any kept line that another kept line "contains" would also delete a database's legitimately repeated or prefix-shared line. Whatever rule lands, a part whose two lines merely SHARE A PREFIX keeps both.

**What this adds that its sibling admin-window/BUG-0179 does not:** BUG-0179 makes the account's derivation EVEN — the same three questions asked of the code, of a blank account and of every line of a multi-line part. This ticket adds nothing to that derivation and asks no question at all: it is a repetition the DATABASE's own words arrive in, deduplicated inside one part after the questions have been asked. If the two are ever worked together, this half lands last and its prefix-sharing twin is what keeps the dedup honest.

## Acceptance criteria
ONE SENTENCE, ONCE. On the measured transport shape — the wrapper `message`, and a `details` carrying the wrapper line, a `Caused by: Error: getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND db.invalid` line, that same sentence again as the head of `cause.stack`, and at least one V8 frame — the account states the cause sentence exactly once and still contains it, still names the object that was read, still states the frame count in the app's own words, and still carries no "node:dns:", no "GetAddrInfoReqWrap" and no absolute path. Measure the account on the pre-ticket tree and record the exact string in the ticket before changing a line: the builder fixes what it measured, not what this description reports.

NOTHING ELSE MOVES. The dedup added is line-level INSIDE one part and nothing else about the derivation changes: the three questions stay three, in their order and their text, no length cap and no vocabulary is introduced, and `withoutSecrets` still runs last. The ten-fixture table of tests/offline/db/result.test.ts keeps its five-and-five shape with every carries/never assertion unchanged; it("says the wrapper once, not twice, when details repeats it"), it("carries the cause out of details when the message is only a wrapper") and BUG-0170's and BUG-0179's pins are green with no case rewritten.

THE TWIN THAT FORBIDS THE GREEDY RULE (LESSONS 8). A named case carries both directions: the repeated cause sentence, said once — and a part whose two lines SHARE A PREFIX but are not the same line (a database message wrapped onto a second line that starts with the first line's opening words), where BOTH lines still cross whole. A rule that deletes a line another line merely begins with fails the twin.

it("says a repeated cause sentence once, and a prefix-sharing line twice") exists in tests/offline/db/result.test.ts as a plain `it`.
