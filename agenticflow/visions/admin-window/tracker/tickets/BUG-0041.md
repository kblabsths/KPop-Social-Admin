# Claims: the all-claims table is unwindowed (877 rows, 30,079px), so the pending-claims gauge moves with the data

## Description
Found on the M1 endgame designer walk (2026-09-03), walk instance :8771 against staging, 1440x900.

`/claims` renders every claim the view holds in one table. Measured on staging: 877 rows, `document.documentElement.scrollHeight` = **30,079px** — 33 viewport-heights — and 12,494 DOM nodes on the page. `h2 PENDING CLAIMS GAUGE` sits at **y=29,486**.

Two rules this breaks:

1. **Feel, Interaction principles, "Repeat use"**: "The operator sees these pages every morning. Nothing moves between visits, counts sit in fixed positions." The pending-claims gauge's position is a function of the claim count: at 877 claims it is at y=29,486; when the resolver drains the backlog to 100 it will be around y=3,500. A gauge whose scroll position is decided by the number below it is never in a fixed position, and the operator relearns where it is every morning.

2. **The app's own window convention.** Every other long list on every other page states its bound in the app's voice — "a window of at most 200, not a count of the cycles that exist" (`/cycles`), "a window of at most 1,000 rows, not the whole table" (four gauges), "The 50 newest events by arrival, newest first — a window, not the whole catalog" (`/browse`). The all-claims list is the ONE list with no such sentence, and it is the one list that is actually unbounded. Its subtitle says only "Oldest first — the longest-waiting claim at the top; a claim whose instant is unknown sorts last."

The bucket table above it is correct and answers Feel bar 1 (every bucket with its count, above the fold at y=184). This ticket is not about that.

Evidence: `agenticflow/tracker/evidence/designer-walk-m1/claims-light.png`, `claims-dark.png`, `claims-standing-light.png`. Measurements recorded above are from the running app, not from source.

Not prescribing the fix: a stated window (the app's existing idiom), or the gauge moved above the list, would each satisfy the rules. A window must carry the app's honesty sentence — a silent truncation would be worse than the current state.

## Acceptance criteria
The all-claims list either states its bound in the same voice the other five surfaces use ("a window of at most N ... not the whole table/view"), or is not the thing that decides where the gauge sits.

`/claims` against staging's current 877 claims renders a page whose named sections (`Buckets`, `All claims`, `Pending claims gauge`) each sit at a position that does not change by thousands of pixels when the claim count changes. State the measured `scrollHeight` and the gauge heading's y in the ticket History, before and after.

No count on the page becomes a lie: if a window is introduced, the bucket table's counts still equal the classification view's per bucket and per filter (EC5 parity holds), and the window sentence names the cap.

A targeted offline test in `tests/offline/claims/page.test.ts` pins whatever bound is chosen (row cap, or the section order) so it cannot silently regress.

Human-check: open `/claims` at 1440x900 and reach the pending-claims gauge without a scroll that reads as a mistake. (human-checkable)
