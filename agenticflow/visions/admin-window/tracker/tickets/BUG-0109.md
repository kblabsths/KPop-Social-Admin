# Bar 13's other half is unbuilt: no windowed list says whether it filled, so five adapter runs that are the whole history read like the top of a long one

## Description
Measured on the M2 endgame walk, 2026-09-09, 1440x900, reading each surface's `data-window-*` hooks beside its rendered sentence.

**Expected** — LOOK_AND_FEEL quality bar 13: "**Every windowed list states its window on screen** — which read produced the rows, the cap it carried, **and whether it filled** — including where the window's bottom is the data's own floor rather than its cap: a list that did **not** fill its cap has the oldest row the object holds, and its line says so ('runs recorded since 2026-08-31; nothing earlier is retained') instead of letting a three-day history read like a long one." The bar records why: "Both M1 sims named this behaviour as the reason they trusted the tool, and the one place it was missing is the only number a sim could not check."

**Found** — `src/components/ui/window-line.tsx` has a `truncated === true` clause on three of its four arms and **no `truncated === false` clause anywhere**. So a window that did not fill says nothing about its floor, and one arm says nothing either way:

| surface | limit | held | truncated | the sentence on screen | missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/cycles` Adapter runs | 200 | **5** | false | "The adapters' newest runs, newest first — a window of at most 200, not a count of the runs that exist." | those five rows **are** every run recorded; the page lets 8-days-of-history read as the top of a long list |
| `/cycles` Cycles | 200 | 69 | false | "The resolver's newest cycles, newest first — a window of at most 200, not a count of the cycles that exist." | same: the oldest row (6d ago) is the object's own floor and the line does not say so |
| `/browse` Recent events | 50 | **50** | **true** | "The 50 newest events by arrival, newest first — a window, not the whole catalog." | whether it filled — the `catalog` arm has no truncated clause at all, so a filled window and an unfilled one render the identical sentence |
| `/` Dashboard, Cycles panel | 6 | 6 | (no hook) | "The resolver's newest cycles, newest first — a window of 6, not a count. Open Cycles & runs for the rest." | filled: not stated |
| `/` Dashboard, Runs panel | 6 | **5** | (no hook) | "The adapters' newest runs, newest first — a window of 6, not a count. Open Cycles & runs for the rest." | not filled: not stated — **and there is no "rest"**; `/cycles` shows the same five runs |

The Dashboard's two panels are the only windowed lists in the app that publish no `data-window-*` hooks at all: they are hand-written prose predating `WindowLine` (DEBT-0003/DEBT-0006 folded the other nine and did not reach these two), so the rule is unenforceable on exactly those two surfaces — the same gap DEBT-0006's docstring records for `/queues`.

Time-bounded **scans** are not in scope and are correct as they stand: `queue_health`, `pending`, `awaiting_row`, `rejections`, `cycle_health` and `resolution_latency` all state `since` and `until`, so their bottom is explained by the time bound rather than by the row count, and `resolution_latency` (held 1,000 of 1,000) already says "The window filled its cap, so every count here is a floor."

Provenance checked before filing: `git log src/components/ui/window-line.tsx src/app/page.tsx` returns ticket-bearing commits only (TASK-0055, TASK-0058, DEBT-0006, DEBT-0003, abe7bb2, TASK-0039, …). No human-lane divergence; violation, not reconciliation.

Evidence: `agenticflow/tracker/evidence/M2/designer/cycles-light-1440.png`, `browse-light-1440.png`, `dashboard-light-1440.png`.

**Sibling note (BUG-0106, BUG-0108).** All three touch `src/app/page.tsx`, found on the same walk, and none touches a line of another. BUG-0106 changes the **colour of an outcome badge** in the Dashboard's cycles table; BUG-0108 changes the **ink and decoration of anchors** on the same page; this one replaces the two hand-written **window paragraphs above** those tables with the shared `WindowLine`. Three different regions of one page file. Land in any order; the only overlap is a rebase.

## Acceptance criteria
1. `WindowLine`'s drawn arms gain a **not-filled** clause: when `truncated` is false, the sentence says the bottom of the list is the object's own floor and not the window's cap, naming the oldest row it holds — bar 13's own form, "runs recorded since <date>; nothing earlier is retained". Walkable on `/cycles`: the Adapter runs line, at 5 rows against a cap of 200, says so.
2. The `catalog` arm (`/browse`) states whether it filled, in both directions: at held 50 of a cap of 50 it says the window filled and there are older events it does not show; below the cap it says the bottom is the catalog's own floor.
3. Every windowed list in the app publishes the `data-window-limit` / `data-window-held` / `data-window-truncated` hooks, **including the Dashboard's two panels**, which stop carrying hand-written prose and render `WindowLine` like the other nine surfaces. Zero windowed lists remain without the hooks.
4. The Dashboard's runs panel stops promising a remainder it does not have: the "Open Cycles & runs for the rest" clause appears only when there is a rest (`truncated` true, or held below the object's total).
5. The line still **follows the read, not the rows** (DECISIONS 2026-09-04): a read that returned keeps its line at zero rows; a read that was refused, absent or never made renders no line at all and the surface's state says why. The absence suite's existing assertions do not change.
6. Time-bounded scans are untouched: their `since`/`until` sentence and the existing "filled its cap, so every count here is a floor" clause render exactly as they do now.
7. The lines stay one `body` paragraph in secondary ink — no new component, no new type step, no colour.
8. `npm run lint` and `tsc --noEmit` clean; the targeted offline files green; the three live page suites green.
