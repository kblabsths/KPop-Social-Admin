## Description

Three residuals QA left unfiled across three lanes, folded into ONE ticket because each is a line or two and they are one class: a fact with two spellings, or a sentence describing a state that is no longer true. Their destinations are disjoint, so one builder session lands all three.

It says two of the six scan-arm call sites - /sources' trend and rejection lines - "are the same defect one page over, unfixed here and reported in this ticket's handoff rather than silently changed". (src/lib/gauges/standing-disagreements.ts:134, from the TASK-0060 builder). `unnamedSources` counts `sourceById.get(sourceId) === undefined`, while every ROW of the same gauge gets its label from `sourceLabel`/`sourceNamesOf` in `src/lib/sources/names.ts`, the one owner of that question (`src/lib/db/review-item.ts:307` states the rule: never a `??` spelled here). Two definitions of "unnamed" in one gauge is the shape LESSONS 5 and 11 describe; the blank-source-name class already cost five tickets (BUG-0152/54/56/58/59) and the strategist ruled no sixth PER-SITE bug - this is the class site, fixed once, at the owner.

3. A COUNT OF 1 READS AS A PLURAL (src/components/ui/window-line.tsx:941, `${count(info.held)} ${of} in all`, and the same phrase at src/app/queues/page.tsx:506; QA residual on BUG-0197, pre-existing). The WORDS stay the designer's - this ticket changes agreement, not voice, and invents no new sentence.
