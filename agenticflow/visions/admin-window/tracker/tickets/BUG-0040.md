# Cycles & runs: the newest adapter run sits 4,419px below the fold (Feel bar 1)

## Description
Found on the M1 endgame designer walk (2026-09-03), walk instance :8771 against staging, viewport 1440x900, light and dark.

LOOK_AND_FEEL.md Feel bar 1 names this page by name: "The question is answered above the fold at 1440x900 without scrolling: ... **Cycles & runs shows the newest run with its counts and error**". (VISION: "did anything happen last night".)

Measured on the running app: `/cycles` renders `h1 CYCLES & RUNS` at y=16, `h2 CYCLES` at y=52 — and `h2 ADAPTER RUNS` at **y=4419**, with `CYCLE HEALTH` at y=4726 and `RESOLUTION LATENCY` at y=5420. Total page height 6,030px. The fold is 900px, so the newest run is 4.9 viewport-heights down: the operator must scroll past up to 200 cycle rows to reach the first row of the runs table.

The cycles half of the bar is met (newest cycle, its counts and its error summary are all above the fold). Only the runs half fails, and it fails because the two sections are stacked and the first one is unbounded-ish (a 200-row window). The Dashboard gets this right — it shows a 6-row window of cycles AND a 6-row window of runs, both above the fold — so the app already knows the shape of the answer; `/cycles` just orders it so the second table is unreachable at a glance.

Evidence: `agenticflow/tracker/evidence/designer-walk-m1/cycles-light.png` (fold view: nothing but cycles), `cycles-runs-section.png` (the runs section, scrolled to), `cycles-dark.png`.

Not prescribing the fix — putting the two newest-run rows beside the cycles window, capping the cycles table at the fold with the rest below, or swapping the section order would all satisfy the bar. What must not happen is dropping the 200-row cycles window; the "a window of at most 200, not a count" honesty is a separate promise the page keeps well.

## Acceptance criteria
At 1440x900 on `/cycles`, the newest adapter run — its source, its start, its outcome and its error — is readable without scrolling, at the same time as the newest cycle's counts and error. (Feel bar 1.)

The page keeps its window honesty: whatever list is shown still states "a window of at most N, not a count", and no count is invented.

A targeted offline test in `tests/offline/cycles/page.test.ts` pins the property structurally — e.g. the rendered markup places the adapter-runs section's first data row within the first screenful's worth of markup, or the cycles table's above-fold portion is bounded — so a later reorder cannot silently push runs back down.

Human-check: on `/cycles` at 1440x900, without scrolling, name the source and outcome of the newest adapter run. (human-checkable)
