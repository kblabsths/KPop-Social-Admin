# An empty claims window ranks the 0 rows it drew and apologises for withholding the rest

## Description
**The sentence.** Where a `/claims` window's COUNT read answers a number and its
ROW read draws none, the window line above the empty card reads, verbatim:

> Oldest first — the longest-waiting claim at the top; a claim whose instant is
> unknown sorts last. A window of at most 50 rows, not the whole view. **900
> claims in all; the 0 longest-waiting are below — the rest are not shown.**

and the card directly under it says the read found nothing. (Reproduced through
the real component at cap 50 / held 900 / drawn 0; byte-pinned today at
`tests/offline/ui/primitives.test.ts:1762`.)

**Why it is filed as a VOICE defect and not a truth defect.** The architect
ruled on 2026-09-11, at BUG-0186's close, that both numbers are facts a read
established and no clause asserts a relationship neither read established — so
this is not the class BUG-0174/0183/0186 fixed, and BUG-0186 left the sentence
byte-identical on purpose so this walk would grade one sentence rather than a
moving one. I graded it, at 1440x900, and it fails.

**What it fails.** LOOK_AND_FEEL → The Voice → Register: *"it talks like the
pipeline's engineer reading you its own logbook — plain, specific, no
reassurance"*. No engineer reading you their logbook says "**the 0
longest-waiting are below**". The clause ranks a set with nothing in it, and
then apologises for withholding "the rest" directly above a card whose whole job
is to say the read came back empty. Read aloud it is two sentences arguing:
the line says rows are being held back from you, the card says there were none.

**The count clause is sound and stays.** "900 claims in all" is a fact the count
read established, and an operator who sees an empty card above a nine-hundred
claim count learns something real from it — that the two reads disagree. What
has to go is the ranking-and-withholding clause when the window drew NO rows at
all: a window that drew none has nothing to rank and nothing to hold back. This
is a sayability rule the line can carry with no new read — `below` is already in
hand at `src/components/ui/window-line.tsx:912` (`const below = onScreen(info)`),
and the clause is composed one line later (:915).

**The pins this will redden, and why they are in this ticket's scope**
(architect's ruling, 2026-09-11, recorded in `agenticflow/tracker/milestones/M3.md`):
the sentence is byte-pinned in exactly one file —
`tests/offline/ui/primitives.test.ts`, case *"leaves every state but the two the
ruling moves byte-identical"* — at **:1759** (the `37` variant), **:1762** (the
`0` variant this ticket moves) and **:1801** (the paged arm's). Those pins were
BUG-0186's proof that it moved two arms and nothing else; QA has since measured
that proof cross-tree, so they have spent their purpose and now hold only the
designer's own copy still. `tests/offline/claims/page.test.ts` already grades
this line digit-masked and pins no word of it.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A `/claims` window whose count read answers a non-zero number and whose row
   read drew **no rows** renders the count clause and **not** the
   ranking-and-withholding clause: the line states what the count read found and
   stops. The empty card below it is unchanged and remains the surface's
   statement that the read found nothing.
2. Every other state of this arm is byte-identical to what it renders today,
   including the one this ticket is nearest to: a window that drew 37 of a
   counted 900 still reads "900 claims in all; the 37 longest-waiting are below
   — the rest are not shown." One arm moves.
3. No new read, no new prop and no new `DrawnWindow` field: the decision is made
   from `below` (`src/components/ui/window-line.tsx:912`), which the line
   already holds.
4. **The byte pins are converted in the same lane.** The three pins in
   `tests/offline/ui/primitives.test.ts` (:1759, :1762, :1801) become STRUCTURAL
   assertions of the same states — the numbers each clause carries, which clauses
   are present, and that the states this ticket does not touch did not move —
   with no word of the product's sentence pinned in that file. A builder that
   changes the wording without converting them hands itself a red it is not
   allowed to green by editing a string.
5. The digit-masked grading in `tests/offline/claims/page.test.ts` still passes
   unchanged, and still pins no word of this line.
6. Human-check: on a fixture (or a forced read) where the count answers 900 and
   the rows come back empty, the screen above the empty card carries no
   superlative and no "the rest are not shown". **(human-checkable)**
