# CYCLE HEALTH renders four calm zeros over a window that holds nothing, two inches under 69 cycles it drops

## Description
Filed from BOTH M3 user-sim walks, independently, 2026-09-11 — the only finding the two strangers hit in the same words.

Marisa (`agenticflow/tracker/for-human/M3-usersim-marisa.md`), who built the pipeline:

> "**CYCLE HEALTH says: cycles in this window `0`, facts examined `0`, writes `0`, errors `0`, '0 cycles reported one,' 'No cycles in this window.'** That block sits maybe two inches under a table listing 69 cycles, five of which died and one of which carries the error `canonical write refused: public.venues holds no row a78dc1c7-…`. The caption does say *'Cycles started since 2026-09-04 17:01 UTC'* and my cycles are all from 09-02/09-03, so the zeros are arithmetically correct. That doesn't help. A wall of `0`s under a heading that says HEALTH reads as *good news* at a glance, and it's the opposite of good news."

Tomas (`…/M3-usersim-tomas.md`), who came to catch the app lying and did not:

> "It is arithmetically correct and its window is named precisely. It still reads, at a glance, as 'everything is fine, nothing is happening' stacked on top of 'here are 69 cycles and some database errors'. If I were checking this page in a hurry I would take away the wrong impression and the page would not have lied to me to do it."

**Expected** — LOOK_AND_FEEL → The Feel → Interaction principles → Zeroes: *"a zero states what it excludes whenever the excluded set is not empty … and never stands bare beside the rows it silently drops."* And the four states: a surface that can render rows renders the **Empty** state — *"naming what the surface will hold and the one thing that fills it"* — rather than a figure derived from a set with no members.

**Found** — `src/components/cycles/cycle-health.tsx` composes `empty` (`holds: "cycles in this window"`, `filledBy: "The resolver wakes on its cron and files a row, and the window fills."`) at `health.cycles === 0` and hands it **only to the two `Distribution`s**. The four `GaugeCard`s above them render regardless, each with a derived sub-line: `0 ran longer than the 15m cadence`, `0 held, and a held fact writes nothing`, `0 applied, 0 escalated, 0 created`, `0 cycles reported one`. So one panel answers the same emptiness two ways in two inches: its distributions say the window holds nothing, its four figures report measurements of it. The panel already owns the honest words and does not use them where the eye lands first.

My own M3 endgame walk graded the Zeroes principle PASS on this exact panel (`agenticflow/tracker/milestones/M3.md`, Design walk, Feel table) on the reasoning that each figure names its own window and neither pretends to be the other. That reasoning is right about truth and wrong about the bar: the bar is about a zero standing beside rows it drops, and 69 such rows are on the same screen. Two document-blind strangers read it the way the bar predicts. This is the walk correcting itself, which is what the blind walks are for.

**What this ticket may NOT do, and why it is written here rather than left to the builder:**
- it may not state how many cycles fall outside the window — that relates two reads and M3 EC7 forbids a rendered sentence asserting a relationship no single read established;
- it may not colour, rank or judge staleness — no staleness threshold exists in this app and DECISIONS 2026-09-02 forbids inventing one (whether the window should widen when the pipeline is stale is Ben's, recorded in the judgment note, not this ticket's);
- it may not change the window line above, which is correct and names its interval.

Provenance checked before filing: `git log src/components/cycles/cycle-health.tsx` returns ticket-bearing commits only (BUG-0116, BUG-0110, BUG-0055, DEBT-0004). Not a human-lane divergence.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Where the cycle-health read found **no cycles in its window** (`health.cycles === 0`), the panel does not render the four `GaugeCard` figures or their derived sub-lines. In their place stands the panel's Empty state, built from the `EmptyWords` the panel already composes — what the surface holds ("cycles in this window") and the one thing that fills it ("The resolver wakes on its cron and files a row, and the window fills.") — so the same emptiness is stated once, not answered two ways on one screen.
2. The `WindowLine` above is untouched: it still names the interval the read carried ("Cycles started since …"), and it is still the first thing under the heading.
3. Where the window holds **one or more** cycles, the panel is byte-identical to what it renders today: four cards, all four sub-lines, both distributions, the duration note and the newest-error line.
4. No new read, no figure from another read, and no sentence relating this window to the table above it (M3 EC7). No colour change, no tone change, no severity, no staleness word: the gray Empty state, as the four-states rule draws it.
5. The two `Distribution`s keep their own empty arms exactly as they are; this ticket adds the missing arm above them and changes nothing below.
6. The offline suite grades it at the surface: a test named `renders no health figure where the window holds no cycles` (that string, so the check below can find it) asserts, on a fixture whose health window is empty, that the four figures are absent and the empty words are present — and, on a fixture with cycles, that all four are present with their sub-lines.
7. Human-check: at 1440x900 on staging, `/cycles` shows no `0` health figure above a table of 69 cycles; what stands there says the window holds no cycles and what would fill it. **(human-checkable)**
