# The five bucket links on /claims are drawn as links only in the DOM: the Badge inside re-inks the words and hides the underline, so the rendered pixels are unchanged

## Description
BUG-0108 converted `src/components/claims/bucket-table.tsx`'s anchor to `IN_PAGE_LINK`. The anchor's computed style now satisfies criterion 1 — but the anchor's whole body is a `<Badge>`, and a Badge sets its own ink (`TONE.neutral = "text-ink"`, `src/components/ui/badge.tsx:15`) on an `inline-block` box with its own `bg-chrome` fill. CSS gives the descendant's ink priority over the inherited accent, and the badge's own background paints over the ancestor's underline. Result: the five bucket names on `/claims` render at rest EXACTLY as they did before BUG-0108 — the fix is invisible on the surface it was filed for.

**Expected** (BUG-0108 criterion 1): "Every anchor inside `main` on ... `/claims` (both tabs) ... renders at rest as this app spells a link: accent ink plus an underline, no hover required." And LOOK_AND_FEEL's bar-10 clause: a thing that navigates says it navigates without the pointer.

**Found**, measured in bundled Chromium on a `next build` of run/admin-window at facd060, served on 127.0.0.1:8901 against staging, viewport 1440x900, mouse parked at (0,0), 400ms after load:

| element | computed | rendered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `main a[data-bucket]` (the anchor) | `color: rgb(152, 16, 250)`, `text-decoration-line: underline` | — |
| its `<span class="type-data inline-block rounded-control bg-chrome px-2 py-0.5 text-ink">` (the glyphs the reader sees) | `color: rgb(30, 41, 57)`, `background-color: rgb(249, 250, 251)`, `text-decoration-line: none`, `display: inline-block` | — |
| crop of the whole anchor, light theme | 0 pixels within 40 of accent `rgb(152,16,250)` | sha1 of the crop is **byte-identical** to the same crop with `style.textDecorationLine='none'` forced on the anchor |
| the same, dark theme | 0 pixels within 40 of accent `rgb(194,122,255)` | byte-identical, 5/5 buckets |
| control — a plain `/browse` title anchor, same treatment | 1000 accent pixels at rest -> 443 with the decoration removed | not identical (the underline is 557 visible pixels) |

All five buckets (`standing_disagreement`, `awaiting_link`, `awaiting_row`, `escalated`, `agreeing`), both themes: `identical_without_underline: true`, `accent_px_at_rest: 0`. Raw measurements: `agenticflow/tracker/evidence/BUG-0108/walk1.json`, `walk2.json` (script `walk2.py`).

These five are the buckets tab's primary navigation — clicking a bucket narrows the claim list — so this is the exact bar-10 cost BUG-0108 was filed to remove, on the page the ticket said carried the most routes off it.

**Repro**
1. `next build && next start` on a free port with staging credentials; sign in with a minted walker cookie.
2. Open `/claims` at 1440x900, do not move the mouse.
3. `getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('main a[data-bucket] span')).color` -> `rgb(30, 41, 57)`; `.textDecorationLine` -> `none`.
4. Screenshot the anchor's bounding box; set `a.style.textDecorationLine='none'`; screenshot again. The two PNGs are byte-identical.

**Why the builder's stated reason does not hold.** The handoff says re-inking the chip is "a shape/colour change criterion 6 forbids". Criterion 6 forbids a change to "ink weight, row height, cell padding or table density" and says explicitly "this changes colour and decoration only" — the badge's ink colour is the one thing criterion 6 leaves open. A fix is available inside the criteria as written (give the badge the link's ink when it is the body of a link, or drop the badge for the bucket name). Note for whoever takes it: `src/components/ui/badge.tsx:4` states "A badge is never interactive" — a bucket name that navigates may not be the right thing to draw as a badge at all, and that judgment belongs with the designer before a builder picks an ink.

**Pin.** `tests/offline/claims/page.test.ts`, "draws the bucket link so the WORDS carry the link's ink, not just the anchor" — a strict `it.fails` pin (it XPASSes red the day this is fixed). It asserts through `expectLinkSpellingReachesTheGlyphs` in `tests/fixtures/link-spelling.ts`, which reads `IN_PAGE_LINK` rather than repeating any class literal. Watched red before pinning: `expected [ 'text-ink' ] to deeply equal []` on `standing_disagreement`.
