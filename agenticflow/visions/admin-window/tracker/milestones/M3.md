## The first ticket of the milestone is a contract amendment, not a page

`ARCHITECTURE.md` §4.3 currently reads *"Paging is not the answer to a cap and
none is built: nothing in the spec asks for it, and complete-or-refuse means
there is never a partial page to continue."* The spec now asks for it (SPEC
F14). §4 rule 1 forbids a component fetching; §5 makes the page function the
only `async` component on a route.

**The architect amends those three contracts before any page changes, and states
in the amendment where the boundary now is.** A builder that finds a contract in
its way files a blocked question; it never interprets one. A page diff that
lands before the amendment is a defect regardless of whether it works.

## Walk notes for the M3 endgame — recorded, NOT filed (architect, 2026-09-11)

The M3 early walk (`agenticflow/tracker/for-human/M3-early-walk.md`) deliberately
recorded two things as non-tickets (1 and 2 below); item 3 was added at
BUG-0175's close, from a QA residual the architect ruled rather than filed.
They are parked here so the endgame walker
and the strategist find them, and so the next walker does not re-file them.

**1. A proposed bar for the endgame — the designer's proposal, in the designer's
words.** *"A control that extends a list is still on screen after it acts."*
Measured on `/claims`: the button's top moves 434 → 2,084 with the viewport 900
tall and `scrollY` unchanged, and the document grows 2,814 → 4,464 — so a press
puts the control ~1,650px below the fold, and paging `/claims` to the end is 17
presses and 17 scrolls. The designer's reason for NOT filing it stands: this is
normal "load more" behaviour, no bar in LOOK_AND_FEEL covers it, and filing it
mid-milestone would be inventing taste. **No bar is invented here.** It is a
LOOK_AND_FEEL addition, which is the designer's document and Ben's call: if it is
accepted at the M3 endgame it becomes a bar first, and a ticket only if the paged
surfaces then fail it. Recorded with it, from the same walk: by the time
`/browse` exhausts, the window line BUG-0174 corrects sits ~4,000px above the
operator who paged, so that fix's readership is whoever reloads or shares the
URL. The continuation-line alternative was rejected in writing under BUG-0172
(it leaves the false sentence where the operator reads it first, and it breaks
the one-`[data-window]` rule) and is not reopened.

**2. A measurement trap for the next walker, not a defect.** Tailwind v4's
`transition-colors` includes `outline-color`, so a computed-style read taken
immediately after Tab returns the MID-TRANSITION value — the walk's first read
said gray-800 and the accent focus ring was nearly filed as missing. **Read the
ring ~200 ms after focus.** As built it is correct: `#9810fa`, 2px, 1px offset,
and Enter pages. Recorded here rather than in `agenticflow/docs/LESSONS.md` on
purpose: LESSONS is the BUILDER's briefing, rewritten whole each close and put in
front of every builder by `ticket.py packet`, and a walker's measurement
technique has no builder application — it would cost a line every builder reads
to serve the one agent who never reads that file.

**3. The paging refusal's em dash at narrow widths — recorded, NOT a defect
(architect ruling, 2026-09-11, off QA's close of BUG-0175).** QA measured, on a
production build against staging: below ~900px the em dash that separates the
identifier from the reason heads a wrapped line with nothing to its left; at
1440px it sits inline with an 8px gap. The mechanism is known and is a
consequence of a change QA graded CORRECT: BUG-0175's builder moved the em dash
into the SANS span so `dir="ltr"` isolates exactly the identifier, and
`Refusal`'s container is `flex flex-wrap items-baseline gap-2`
(`src/components/ui/paging.tsx`), so the sans span wraps as one flex item and
takes its leading em dash to the head of the new line. The 8px is `gap-2`.

**Ruled: not a violation, and not to be filed.** LOOK_AND_FEEL names a minimum
supported width, and ~900px is below it. `agenticflow/docs/vision/LOOK_AND_FEEL.md:133-140`:
*"Viewport: the frame is desktop-only, 1280px wide and up. Supported range is
1280px to no upper bound; the design target is 1440x900 (bar 1). ... Below
1280px the app is unsupported in M1, and nothing a narrow viewport does is a
violation"* — Ben ruled that 2026-09-02, and it already covers a worse
measurement (the Dashboard's attention counts wrapping one word per line at
390px, BUG-0050, recorded and not a defect). A ~900px reading grades nothing.

**What the endgame walk should do instead: measure it at 1,280 — the supported
FLOOR — and only then.** The two measurements in hand are 1440 (inline, fine)
and ~900 (unsupported, ungraded); the boundary between them is untested, and
1280 is the only width at which this can be a defect. If the em dash heads a
wrapped line at 1280 on any refusal arm, THAT is filable, against
LOOK_AND_FEEL's Voice/Typography rather than against a width bar. Measure after
BUG-0176 lands, not before: its criterion 13 changes what this line is made of
(the not-provisioned arm renders the object as an `<Identifier>` node inside an
extracted clause), so a reading taken today describes markup that is about to
change on one of the five arms.

**4. "The 0 longest-waiting are below" over an empty card — recorded, NOT filed
(architect ruling, 2026-09-11, off QA's close of BUG-0183 and the filing of
BUG-0186).** QA measured on `/claims`: a window whose COUNT read answers 900
while its row read comes back with none renders "900 claims in all; the 0
longest-waiting are below — the rest are not shown." above the empty card
(reproduced through the real component on a988b00: cap 50, held 900, drawn 0).

**Ruled: not a truth defect, so not a BUG-0186 criterion.** Both numbers are
facts a read established — 900 is the count read's, 0 is the rows the page drew
— and no clause asserts a relationship between them that neither read
established, which is the rule BUG-0174/BUG-0183/BUG-0186 enforce. What is
wrong with it is that it RANKS an empty set and then apologises for withholding
the rest above a card that already says the read found nothing: a wording
problem, and wording is the designer's jurisdiction (criteria and DEBT never pin
UI copy). BUG-0186 leaves this line byte-identical on purpose (its criterion 4a
pins it) so that the endgame walker grades one sentence rather than a moving
one.

**What the endgame walk should do: grade it against the Voice bars at 1440x900,
after BUG-0186 lands**, and if it fails, file it as a Voice defect naming the
empty-card duplication — not as a window-line truth defect. The count clause
itself is sound; a candidate the designer may prefer is to drop the ranking
clause when the window drew no rows at all, which is a sayability rule the
window line can carry without any new read.

**The pin that fix will redden, and who carries it (architect ruling,
2026-09-11, off QA's close of BUG-0186).** That sentence is byte-pinned in
exactly ONE place: `tests/offline/ui/primitives.test.ts`, in the case *"leaves
every state but the two the ruling moves byte-identical"* — line 1709 for the
`0` variant QA flagged, line 1706 for the `37` one, line 1748 for the paged
arm's. Measured 2026-09-11 on run/admin-window: `grep -rn longest-waiting tests
src` returns three byte pins, all in that file; `tests/offline/claims/page.test.ts`
already grades this line digit-masked (`shapeOf`, `replace(/[\d,]+/g, "#")`)
and pins no word of it. BUG-0186's criterion 4 mandated those pins to prove the
ticket moved two arms and nothing else, and QA has since measured that proof
cross-tree (one-file differential, exactly 2 reds) — so the pins have SPENT
their purpose and now only hold the designer's own copy still.

**Ruled: the Voice BUG this walk files carries
`tests/offline/ui/primitives.test.ts` in its `touch_scope`, and its criteria
say the pin is converted to a STRUCTURAL assertion — the numbers, the hooks and
the presence of the two facts, with no word of the sentence pinned — in the
same lane as the wording change.** Both halves in one ticket; a walker that
files the wording fix without that scope line hands its builder a red it is not
allowed to touch. No separate de-pin DEBT is filed ahead of the walk, on
purpose: it would cost a builder lane to save one scope line, and it would land
in `tests/offline/ui`, which is inside **TASK-0072**'s touch scope (open, M3,
and TASK-0073 depends on it) — two tickets writing one destination for no gain.
If the walk grades the sentence GOOD and files nothing, the pins simply stand,
blessed.

**5. The bound-ceiling sentence's next step is claims-shaped on `/browse` —
recorded, NOT filed (architect, 2026-09-11, off QA's close of BUG-0178).** The
limit arm of `PageMore` (`src/components/ui/paging.tsx:483`,
`stoppedAtTheCeiling`) ends *"Narrow the view and page the smaller set."* The
noun is per-surface (`holds`, so `/browse` correctly reads "events"), but the
NEXT STEP is not: `/browse` offers no facets at all. QA confirmed the mechanism
on the landed tree — `/browse`'s only parameter is `columns`, which "chooses
which COLUMNS render and never which rows are read"
(`src/app/browse/page.tsx:215-221`, `scope: null`) — so the sentence tells the
operator of that page to do a thing the page does not offer.

**Reachability, measured, and it is why nothing is filed:** the arm draws only at
`MAX_PAGE_OFFSET + SIZE` = 100,050 rows, and read-only staging holds **120
events** (against 877 claims). `/browse`'s ceiling is three orders of magnitude
away; no criterion, no receipt and no walk can drive that surface into this arm
on this data. QA reached it only through its own `PagingProvider` +
`PageMore` probe at a typed `held`, which is a component reading, not a page
reading. It is not test-pinnable either without grading prose, which criteria and
DEBT may not do (wording is the designer's jurisdiction).

**What the endgame walk should do: grade the sentence against the Voice bars at
1440x900, on the ONE surface where it is reachable (`/claims`, ceiling
100,000 claims) and on the component reading for `/browse`, and file a Voice BUG
only if it FAILS** — the fact that a next step is unreachable advice on a
surface the operator cannot reach is a defect only if the bars say it is. If it
passes, this note is the blessing and the next walker does not re-file it.

**The cost, if it is filed, so the walk files it with its scope right:** a
per-surface next step needs a NEW PROP on `PageMore` (a next-step string or a
"this surface can be narrowed" flag) threaded from **both** call sites —
`src/components/claims/paged-claim-list.tsx:94` and
`src/components/browse/paged-browse-table.tsx:165` — both of which were outside
BUG-0178's touch scope, which is exactly why its builder left the gap rather
than widening. A Voice BUG here therefore carries `src/components/ui/paging.tsx`
AND both wrappers AND `tests/offline/ui/paging.test.ts` in its touch scope; one
without the wrappers hands its builder a sentence it cannot make per-surface.
Cheaper alternative the designer may prefer, and which needs no prop: a next
step true of every surface, or none at all — the arm's other two sentences
already stand on their own.
