# M2 — the verdict slice, and the campaign's last milestone

Campaign `admin-window`. Slice definition: `agenticflow/docs/vision/ROADMAP.md`.
Behavior: `agenticflow/docs/vision/SPEC.md` (F9–F13, added 2026-09-04).
Acceptance authority: `contracts/admin-build.md`, read 2026-09-01 — its test
numbers are cited as **[test N]**.

**M2 carries zero *installed* schema.** It authors two migrations as handoff
artifacts and installs neither. No migration lands in this repo, no DDL runs
from app code, and no surface may assume `verdicts` or `settle_review_item`
exists. If a criterion below appears to require installed schema, it is
mis-authored — report it, do not install anything.

**The absence is the normal case.** `main` deploys against a database without
those two objects for the whole of M2 and beyond, until Ben installs them. Every
surface M2 builds renders its not-provisioned state there, offers no action it
cannot perform, and does not throw. That is not a fallback; it is the graded
behavior.

**No dials.** Nothing in M2 renders, reads, hand-copies, or is shaped like a
threshold control. Ben builds dials himself after this campaign closes.
