# The Dashboard's attention zeros never say what fills the queue, and one of them speaks for the whole pipeline

## Description
Filed from the M2 user-sim walk of Devin (`agenticflow/tracker/for-human/M2-usersim-devin.md`), 2026-09-09. It is the first thing he read and the second-biggest thing that stopped him.

> §1: "OPEN DECISIONS **0** — 'nothing open — no question is waiting on a verdict' … In ten seconds I knew the shape of my afternoon: one item, high, six days old, and **nobody is waiting on a verdict from me**."
>
> §3, forty minutes later, on Claims: "**The dashboard said zero decisions are waiting on a verdict, and there are 108 links nobody can make.** From where I sit those are in tension … Either the system isn't asking, or the asking mechanism is the same missing `verdicts` table. I could not tell which from any page, and that distinction is exactly what determines whether this is 'wait for Marisa' or 'wake someone up.'"
>
> §7, his second of three blockers: "'OPEN DECISIONS 0' next to 108 things visibly waiting on a human judgement. I can't tell whether the system isn't asking me, or can't ask me yet."

**Expected** — LOOK_AND_FEEL Voice copy bar 4: "Every empty and not-provisioned state names what the surface holds and **what fills it**. Never a bare 'No data' … : 'No open decisions — the resolver files one here when sources disagree.'" The doc's own worked example is this exact card.

**Found** — `src/app/page.tsx:163-167`:

```
/** How each kind's empty count reads, and what fills it. */
const NOTHING_OPEN: Record<Kind, string> = {
  decision: "nothing open — no question is waiting on a verdict",
  signal: "nothing open — nothing is reporting a breakage",
};
```

Neither string names what fills the queue; the comment above them says they do. Both restate the emptiness in different words, and the decision one restates it as a claim about the world — "no question is waiting on a verdict" — which a stranger read, reasonably, as "nothing needs a human", on a morning when 877 claims had been held for six days and 108 performers were waiting for a link nobody can make. The card read one table (`review_items`, kind `decision`, open) and spoke for the whole pipeline.

Both were verified on the walk instance (127.0.0.1:8771, staging, 2026-09-09).

**What this ticket is NOT.** It does not ask the Dashboard to relate decisions to held claims, or to colour a six-day-old last-apply amber: the first is a cross-object claim the app cannot substantiate from the reads it makes, and the second needs a staleness threshold that DECISIONS 2026-09-02 forbids this campaign from inventing. The honest remedy is the one copy bar 4 already asks for — say what files a decision, and a reader learns for free that the resolver files them, so an empty queue means the resolver has not filed one rather than "nothing is wrong".

Provenance checked before filing: `git log src/app/page.tsx` returns ticket-bearing commits only (BUG-0115, BUG-0114, BUG-0109, BUG-0108). Not a human-lane divergence.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Each attention card's zero sub-line names **what fills that queue**, in copy bar 4's form and the app's register: what the surface holds, then the one thing that puts a row in it — the resolver files a decision, the resolver files a signal. The doc's own example ("No open decisions — the resolver files one here when sources disagree.") is a legal answer for the decision card.
2. Neither line makes a claim wider than the read behind it. "no question is waiting on a verdict" — a statement about the whole pipeline drawn from one table's open rows — goes.
3. The count itself is unchanged: a counted zero stays a real figure in its fixed position (DECISIONS 2026-09-02), the card stays a link to its queue, and the non-empty rendering (severity word + oldest age) is untouched, ink and hooks included.
4. No new sentence relating decisions to claims, cycles or held facts; no colour change on any zero; no threshold, staleness reading or computed score anywhere on the Dashboard.
5. The words are the same ones the corresponding queue's own empty state uses on `/queues`, or the two are deliberately different and the ticket's History says why — one concept, one spelling (glossary discipline).
6. Human-check (human-checkable): on the walk instance at 1440x900, a reader who has never opened this app can say what would make a decision appear, from the Dashboard alone.
7. `npm run lint` and `tsc --noEmit` clean; the offline suite green.
