# The record page draws a subset of the row's columns and never says so — absent and not-drawn share one rendering

## Description
Filed from both M2 user-sim walks (2026-09-09) — the same ambiguity, found independently on two different tables.

**Priya** (`M2-usersim-priya.md` §7), on a venue record: "The venue record also shows only five fields — `venue_id, name, city, country, address`. The signal page had shown me `venues.timezone Europe/Amsterdam` claimed by ticketmaster, and the claims page lists `venues.latitude`, `venues.longitude`, `venues.timezone` waiting. None of those appear on the venue record at all. **So a fact I was shown on one page has no home on the page that's supposed to be about that record, and I can't tell whether it's absent, unapplied, or just not displayed.**"

**Devin** (`M2-usersim-devin.md` §4), on an event record: "Claims talks about `events.event_type`, `events.status`, `events.time_precision` and `events.ticket_url`, and the record page has no rows for any of them. I couldn't tell whether those columns don't exist yet or the page just doesn't show them — and after the `column \"venue\" does not exist` errors in Cycles, **that ambiguity made me twitchy**."

**Expected** — the Feel's Emptiness principle: two different states never share a rendering ("an empty bucket, a table with no rows, and an unprovisioned table are three different states and never share a rendering"). Here three states share one rendering — nothing on the page: a column that exists and is null, a column the page deliberately does not draw, and a column that is not in the database. And VISION: "every page says so honestly". Every list surface in this app states the scope of its read, in the words both sims named as the reason they trusted it; the record page is the one surface that draws a subset and says nothing about it.

**Found** — the record page draws exactly `mappedColumns(config)`: for `events`, `event_id, title, description, poster_url, starts_at, venue_id`; for `venues`, `venue_id, name, city, country, address` (`src/lib/edit/config.ts:207-263`). That set is Ben's ruling of 2026-09-02 and is correct — this is not a request to widen it. What is missing is one sentence saying that it IS a set. Verified on the walk instance (127.0.0.1:8771, staging, 2026-09-09): `/records/events/01a03f78-a122-7baf-acf7-6f997a030048` renders the h1, the regime note, the `verdicts` card, the dash-in-Provenance note, then a six-row Fields table — no line anywhere about which columns it draws.

The page already has the neighbouring sentence done exactly right, and both sims praised it: "A — in Provenance means no field provenance is recorded for that field: the value has no source behind it, rather than a source this page failed to read." That is this ticket's model, one column over.

Provenance checked before filing: `git log` on `src/app/records/[table]/[id]/page.tsx` and `src/components/records/record-fields.tsx` returns ticket-bearing commits only (BUG-0120, BUG-0112, BUG-0100, BUG-0101, TASK-0055). Not a human-lane divergence.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The record page states the scope of its Fields table in one line, in the app's voice, beside the table (the dash-in-Provenance note is the model and the neighbour): the fields listed are the columns this window deals with for that table, named as a count, and a column not listed is one this page does not draw — not a value the database is missing.
2. The line follows the page's own read, like every window line in this app: it names the table verbatim in mono (Voice bar 5) and states the number of columns drawn. It claims nothing about the columns it does not draw — not how many there are, not their names, not why they are out.
3. It renders on both resolver-owned tables and on the sandbox table, in body sans at the secondary ink, inside the Fields section, above the table — one line, not a card, no colour, no icon.
4. No change to which columns are drawn, their order, their values, their provenance cells, the regime note, the `verdicts` card or any state hook.
5. Human-check (human-checkable): on the walk instance at 1440x900, light and dark, a reader on `/records/venues/<id>` who was just shown a `venues.timezone` claim on the signal page can tell, from the record page alone, that the absence of a timezone row is the page's scope and not the database's silence.
6. `npm run lint` and `tsc --noEmit` clean; the offline suite green.
