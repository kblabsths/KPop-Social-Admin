# /claims and /sources describe the SAME pending-claims window with two different objects — 'not the whole view' vs 'not the whole table'

## Description
DEBT-0003 folded three hand-copied window lines into one `WindowLine` primitive and made the closing noun a REQUIRED `over` prop, whose contract the primitive states in its own docstring (src/components/ui/window-line.tsx:35-39): 'the split is settled by the object rather than by whichever copy won — a window over a view says view, a window over a table says table'. That is also DEBT-0003's acceptance criterion, verbatim. The values that were then passed reproduce the pre-fold split exactly.

**Expected** (DEBT-0003 acceptance criterion; src/components/ui/window-line.tsx:35-39; ARCHITECTURE.md 4.3 read kind 2, 'the surface says which window it is showing'): two surfaces rendering the SAME window describe it identically, and the closing noun names the object the window was read over.

**Found**: /claims's pending-claims gauge and /sources's awaiting-row gauge render one and the same `WindowInfo` — both go through `fetchPendingClaims` (src/lib/gauges/pending-claims.ts:179-203), whose window is `windowOf(bounds, observations.data.length)` (line 200) over `readPendingObservations` → `.from(T.observations)` (src/lib/db/gauges.ts:335-352), i.e. over `observations`, a TABLE (src/lib/db/tables.ts:17) — and describe it with two different nouns:

  /claims  (src/app/claims/page.tsx:303, over="view"):
    'Claims observed since 2026-06-06 12:00 UTC, read to 2026-09-04 12:00 UTC — a window of at most 1,000 rows, not the whole view.'
  /sources (src/app/sources/page.tsx:469, over="table"):
    'Claims observed since 2026-06-06 12:00 UTC, read to 2026-09-04 12:00 UTC — a window of at most 1,000 rows, not the whole table.'

Byte-identical for 100 characters; they differ in the last word only. The standing tab (src/app/claims/page.tsx:382) carries the same over="view" over the same read.

Worse on /claims alone, where two window lines now stand on one page under two different caps and both end 'not the whole view': the LIST line (src/app/claims/page.tsx:135, data-window="claims", limit 50) really is over the `pending_claims` view; the GAUGE line (data-window="pending", limit 1,000) is over `observations`, and the 1,000 cap it announces was never applied to the view at all — the claim leg is an id lookup (readPendingClaimRows), not a 1,000-row scan of the view.

Nothing in the suite pins the value. Sabotage, measured 2026-09-04 on a detached worktree at 5406885: flipping /claims to over="table", and separately flipping /cycles to over="view", each left all 600 tests of tests/offline/{ui/primitives,cycles/page,sources/page,claims/page,review-item/page,absence/pages}.test.ts green. `over` is graded only against itself, inside the primitive's own test.

## What closing it means

Either (a) /claims's gauge `over` follows its object and becomes "table" like /sources', or (b) the architect rules that the gauge window is legitimately described by the view its rows come from, in which case /sources' awaiting-row line — the SAME read — moves with it and `WindowLine`'s docstring is restated to say what actually decides the word. What is not closeable is the two pages disagreeing. Either way the strict pin is retired (`.fails` removed), so the assertion runs for real.
