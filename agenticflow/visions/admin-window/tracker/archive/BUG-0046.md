# Counts do not agree with their noun: '1 sources holding one', 'of 1 items read here', '1 sources, 2 domains'

## Description
Found on the M1 endgame designer walk (2026-09-03), walk instance :8771 against staging. Voice copy bar 6: "counts carry their noun".

Rendered on the running app, verbatim:

- `/sources`, awaiting-row trend, sub-line of the 769 figure: **"1 sources holding one"** (`src/app/sources/page.tsx:509`)
- `/queues`, queue-health entity_link folded card: **"of 1 items read here, 700 folds in all"** (`src/app/queues/page.tsx:296`)
- `/claims`, pending-claims gauge: **"1 sources, 2 domains"** (`src/app/claims/page.tsx:267`) and **"0 sources holding one"** (`:339`)

Every one is `count(n) + " " + <always-plural noun>`. At n=1 the app says "1 sources" and "1 items", which is the one thing a careful operator's eye catches instantly and the one thing that makes a console look unfinished. Staging happens to hold exactly one source with awaiting-row claims and exactly one folded entity_link item, so this is not a hypothetical — it is what the page says today, on two of the six pages.

"1 sources holding one" also reads oddly at any n: "holding one" leaves the operator to work out what "one" refers to (an awaiting-row claim).

Evidence: `agenticflow/tracker/evidence/designer-walk-m1/sources-light.png` (the 769 card), `queues-light.png` (queue-health cards).

Low priority: nothing is unreadable and no number is wrong. It costs one shared helper and a handful of call sites.

**What this adds that its siblings do not.** BUG-0041 (row count), BUG-0043 (source names) and BUG-0045 (two prose sentences) each change a different thing on `/claims`; this one changes only the sub-line of gauge cards, and its real home is a shared helper in `src/lib/format.ts` that none of the siblings touch.

## Acceptance criteria
No rendered string in the app pairs the count 1 with a plural noun. Walk `/sources`, `/queues` and `/claims` against staging's current data and confirm the three strings above now read correctly at n=1.

The rule is applied once, not per call site: a shared helper in `src/lib/format.ts` next to `count()` takes the count and the noun, so a new gauge card cannot reintroduce this.

`tests/offline/format.test.ts` covers 0, 1 and n for that helper.

Human-check: on `/sources` and `/queues`, every count reads as a person would say it aloud. (human-checkable)
