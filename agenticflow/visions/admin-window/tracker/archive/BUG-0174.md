# A continued window still calls itself a 50-row window and denies being the whole set, under 877 rows and "All claims in this view are shown" — /claims and /browse

## Description
BUG-0172 criterion 8 was marked (human-checkable) and deferred to this walk by name: "The exhausted window line and the exhausted paging sentence read as one voice: they agree, and the line neither contradicts nor parrots 'All N in this view are shown'. The designer's walk grades the words." Walked on a production build against staging (own port 8795, my PID, killed; reads only), 1440x900. The words fail it.

BUG-0172 fixed the TRUNCATED clause of the paged window line and left the clause beside it stating the FIRST read. The sentence therefore describes a 50-row window while 877 rows are on screen, and denies being the whole set three lines above a control that says it is.

MEASURED, verbatim, all three paged walks:

1. /claims unnarrowed, 17 presses, 877 claim rows drawn, data-window-held="877", data-window-truncated="false", [data-paging="exhausted"]:
   line: 'Oldest first — the longest-waiting claim at the top; a claim whose instant is unknown sorts last. A window of at most 50 rows, NOT THE WHOLE VIEW. 877 claims in all; THE 877 LONGEST-WAITING are below — the read found no more.'
   control: 'All claims in this view are shown.'

2. /browse, 2 presses, 120 event rows, held="120", truncated="false":
   line: 'The newest events by arrival, newest first — a window of at most 50, NOT THE WHOLE CATALOG. 120 events are on screen, and the read found no more.'
   control: 'All events in this view are shown.'

3. /claims?bucket=awaiting_link, 2 presses, 108 rows, held="108", truncated="false":
   line: '… A window of at most 50 rows, not the whole view. 108 claims match these filters; THE 108 LONGEST-WAITING are below — the read found no more.'

Three defects in one sentence, one fix site (the matched and catalog arms of src/components/ui/window-line.tsx):

(a) THE CAP CLAUSE NEVER FOLLOWED THE PRESS. 'A window of at most 50 rows' / 'a window of at most 50' is said of a screen holding 877, 120 and 108 rows. 50 is now the size of ONE press, not a description of what the operator holds — the same class BUG-0172 fixed for truncated, left standing in the neighbouring clause of the same sentence.

(b) 'NOT THE WHOLE VIEW' / 'NOT THE WHOLE CATALOG' IS THE CONTRADICTION CRITERION 8 NAMES. The control below says 'All claims in this view are shown.' / 'All events in this view are shown.' One page, two answers to one question, in words this time rather than in hooks. On a first screen the clause is true and must stay; on a continued window the read has just said the set ended.

(c) 'THE 877 LONGEST-WAITING ARE BELOW' IS A SUPERLATIVE OVER A SET WITH NOTHING OUTSIDE IT. '877 claims in all; the 877 longest-waiting are below' states one number twice and implies a ranked selection from a larger set that does not exist. Same on the narrowed arm at 108. The register is 'plain, specific' (LOOK_AND_FEEL, The Voice); this clause is neither.

RULES VIOLATED: LOOK_AND_FEEL bar 13 ('Every windowed list states its window on screen — which read produced the rows, the cap it carried, and whether it filled'), The Voice register, and ARCHITECTURE §4.3 kind 3 as amended 2026-09-11 ('The window line of a paged surface states the read the operator NOW HOLDS'). Traces to VISION: 'real staging rows whose numbers match what the database says'.

NOT IN SCOPE: the hooks. data-window-limit, -held and -truncated are all correct and graded green by BUG-0172; no attribute is added or changed, and the first server render stays byte-identical (its cap clause is true on a first screen and must survive to the byte). FEAT-0016's windowed figures are untouched.

## The ruling (architect, 2026-09-11) — one pass fixes the class, and two of BUG-0172's QA residuals fold into it

**Order.** This is the HEAD of the paged-surface chain. Truth defects on a live
surface land before contract work with a declared behaviour of zero, so:
BUG-0174 → BUG-0175 → BUG-0176 (the refusal's anatomy, `Refusal` + the refusal
contract) and BUG-0174 → BUG-0177 → BUG-0178 (`PageMore`: the control's shape,
then the end-of-the-road sentence) are the two lanes after it, and TASK-0071 /
TASK-0072 — chained behind BUG-0172, now closed — are re-chained behind THIS
ticket, because both inherit the arms it rewrites.

**Scope widened to the class.** The words live in `window-line.tsx`'s two paged
arms, but two residuals QA recorded at BUG-0172's close ("no path a criterion or
receipt drives") are the SAME defect one file over — a fact about the operator's
screen that is INFERRED instead of stated — and the sentences this ticket
rewrites choose their numbers off exactly those facts. Fixing the words while
leaving the inference is the shape BUG-0172 already paid for once (it fixed one
clause and left its neighbour). So both fold in here, as explicit facts:

### (a) Whose number `held` is, is a FACT OF THE WINDOW — never a size

`PagedWindowLine` today decides it by comparing:

```tsx
held: first.held <= first.limit ? drawn : first.held,   // src/components/ui/paging.tsx
```

`/browse`'s `held` MEANS "the rows this surface's reads came back with", so a
press grows it. `/claims`' `held` MEANS "the matching set's own count read", and
paging reads no claim into it (it is also the hook `tests/live/claims.live.test.ts`'s
paged-walk oracle grades the walk against). Those are two meanings of one field,
and the component guesses between them by asking which is bigger. It is
unreachable-wrong today only because `/claims` is drawn pageable only when its
count exceeds the cap — and it is a trap for TASK-0071/0072, which inherit these
arms. The window STATES it:

```ts
// src/components/ui/window-line.tsx
export interface DrawnWindow extends WindowFacts {
  …
  /**
   * What `held` COUNTS — stated by the page that composed these facts, never
   * inferred from the number's size.
   *
   *  "this window"  → `held` is the rows THIS window's reads came back with, so
   *                   a press that appends rows grows it (`/browse`).
   *  "a count read" → `held` came from a SEPARATE count over the matching set;
   *                   a press reads no row into it, so it stands (`/claims`,
   *                   whose live paged-walk oracle grades that hook).
   *
   * Absent → "this window": the nine unpaged lines mean exactly that, no call
   * site of them changes, and nothing about them re-renders.
   */
  heldFrom?: "this window" | "a count read";
}
```

`PagedWindowLine` reads it and compares no number to `limit`. Both paged pages
SET it on the first-screen `DrawnWindow` they already compose exactly once
(BUG-0172's standing rule: one composition, handed to whichever component renders
it — two spellings of those facts is how the two come to disagree).
**No `data-window-*` attribute is added**: the hook set is closed and the first
server render stays byte-identical (SPEC F14, §5).

### (b) On a paged surface, no clause takes its number from the CAP

The matched arm falls back to the cap — `const below = continuedTo(info) ?? info.limit`
— so an exhausted `/claims` that appended ZERO rows renders the CAP's number as
the screen's: with a count of 877 and a read that returns 50 and then says no
more, "877 claims in all; the 50 longest-waiting are below — the read found no
more." Reachable through a count/read divergence (the count and the rows are two
reads; staging's count moved 877→878 between two sessions of this very campaign).
On a paged surface `drawn` is always a number and it is the only honest source of
"what is on screen". The cap keeps its one true use: the FIRST screen, where the
cap is what the read drew.

And the divergence itself is stated honestly or not at all: `/claims`' count and
its rows are two reads, so where they disagree each is stated as its own read and
no clause asserts a relationship between them that no single read established
(LESSONS 2). "The set is complete on screen" is not said while naming a count
larger than the rows drawn.

### Not re-opened — BUG-0172 ruled these in writing

A second, client-rendered continuation line below the rows; a rewording that
leaves the hooks disagreeing; a new `data-window-*` attribute; changing
`/claims`' `held` to the row count (it would silently move a live oracle's
expectation). Nothing here needs any of them.

### Words

The designer owns them. The criteria below grade what a sentence may not ASSERT
and which fact each number comes from; no check pins a word of product copy, and
the new sentences use the app's existing nouns. Criterion 11 is the walk's.

## Acceptance criteria
1. On a CONTINUED window (a press has appended rows) neither paged arm states the first read's cap: the rendered text of /claims' matched arm and /browse's catalog arm contains no "at most" clause. Asserted on rendered markup for both arms, in the truncated-after-a-press state AND the exhausted state.
2. On a CONTINUED window the read has said is exhausted, neither arm denies being the whole set: the rendered text contains neither "not the whole view" nor "not the whole catalog", and it does not contradict the [data-paging="exhausted"] sentence rendered below it.
3. Neither arm states one number twice in the same clause: where the rows drawn equal the count the surface holds back nothing, the sentence says the set is complete on screen rather than ranking it ("the N longest-waiting are below" is not said of a set with nothing outside it). The narrowed arm (/claims?bucket=…, "N claims … match these filters") obeys the same rule.
4. WHOSE NUMBER `held` IS, IS A FACT THE WINDOW STATES (BUG-0172 residual (a)). `DrawnWindow` carries it, both paged pages set it on the first-screen facts they compose ONCE, and `PagedWindowLine` renders the line without comparing `held` — or any other number — to `limit` or to a row count beside it. Graded both ways on rendered markup: a window whose held counts its own rows grows with the rows a press appends; a window whose held came from a count read stands unmoved while rows are appended under it. After every press /claims' data-window-held is still the matching count its own count read established and /browse's is still the rows on screen.
5. NO CLAUSE OF A PAGED ARM TAKES ITS NUMBER FROM THE CAP (BUG-0172 residual (b)). In every press state — including an exhausted window that appended ZERO rows, i.e. drawn equal to the first window's rows with the paging state exhausted — the number a sentence names for what is on screen is the rows drawn, never `limit`. Asserted on rendered markup with a /claims fixture whose count (877) exceeds the rows its read returned at exhaustion (50), and on that fixture no clause asserts a relationship between the count and the rows drawn that no single read established: the arm does not say the set is complete on screen while naming a larger count.
6. EVERY PRESS STATE IS GRADED ON BOTH PAGES: offered (idle), loading, refused, refused at the bound ceiling (data-paging="limit") and exhausted. For each of the five, on /claims and on /browse, the window line's rendered text is asserted through the real driver from the real deps the page handed it — and in every one of them the line publishes exactly ONE [data-window] element whose data-window-truncated agrees with the paging state below it.
7. THE FIRST SCREEN IS BYTE-IDENTICAL. With no press, /claims, /claims?bucket=awaiting_link, /claims?tab=standing and /browse render the window line they render today — same element, same position, same attributes, same words, cap clause included — and BUG-0172's four named first-screen cases stand UNMODIFIED in the diff and pass ("publishes the read's own facts, and says the window did not fill" and "says the window filled its cap, and names no floor of its own" on /browse; "states the cap and the number of claims it holds back" and "does not claim to be truncated when every matching claim is drawn" on /claims).
8. The nine unpaged window lines are untouched — /cycles x2, /queues, /sources x2, /review, the Dashboard's two panels and the entity picker render byte-identical lines on their existing fixtures, their suites pass unmodified, and WindowLine called without the new facts renders exactly what it renders today.
9. No data-window-* attribute is added or changed, and "truncated" keeps its ONE derivation (the paging state's status); nothing re-derives it by counting rows.
10. A refused press still settles nothing, on both pages: the window line's markup after a refusal is byte-identical to the line before it, at the bound ceiling included, where it still says rows are not shown.
11. (human-checkable) On a production build against staging, page /claims to the end (17 presses, 877 rows) and /browse to the end (2 presses, 120 rows). The window line and the sentence under the table must read as one voice — the line must not call the screen a 50-row window, must not say it is not the whole view/catalog, and must not say "877 of 877 longest-waiting". The designer's walk grades the words.
12. Lint is clean and a PRODUCTION build is green.
